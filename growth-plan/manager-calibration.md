# Manager Calibration

Use this doc to track alignment conversations with your manager about your career growth.

## How to Use This

Before your next 1:1 or career conversation, run `/prep-1on1` to generate private source notes, manager-safe talking points, and calibration asks from your recent impact notes, feedback you choose to reuse, goals, and prior open questions. Then bring specific questions:

- "Here's what I think I need to demonstrate for [next level]. Does that match your understanding?"
- "I wrote up [recent ship] as an impact note. Would this count as evidence of [competency]?"
- "What's the gap between where I am and where I need to be for [goal]?"

After the conversation, log the key takeaways here so you don't lose them.
