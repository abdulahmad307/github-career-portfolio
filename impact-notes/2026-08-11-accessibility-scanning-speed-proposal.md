# Accessibility Scanning Speed Proposal

**Date:** 2026-08-11

## Work moment

I wrote and shared a proposal with the broader team outlining ways to speed up the daily accessibility scanning workflow and make it less error-prone.

## Why it mattered

The workflow often takes approximately 30 to 40 minutes to complete. That delay slows the feedback loop when the team needs to verify that scanning is working correctly or rerun the process to open or close specific issues.

## What changed

The proposal made the workflow's performance problem and potential improvement approaches visible to the broader team, creating a concrete basis for discussion and future prioritization.

No implementation has been started, so runtime and reliability have not changed yet.
