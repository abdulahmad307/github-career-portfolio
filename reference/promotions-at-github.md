Promotions
Promotions are one way GitHub recognizes and invests in a Hubber’s career growth. They reflect an increase in grade that occurs when a Hubber’s role expands in scope. Throughout the year, managers should have conversations about the skills required at the next level, clearly outlining where the Hubber meets expectations and where further growth is needed.

Promotions are conducted twice per fiscal year with promotion effective dates September 1 and March 1. The promotion philosophy and criteria are the same for all Hubbers and Microsoft Integrated Employees (MIE). 

 

Promotion Criteria
A promotion nomination requires that three criteria be met:

Criteria 1: Employee readiness
Criteria 2: Business need
Criteria 3: Budget availability
Criteria 1: Employee readiness means a Hubber consistently delivers meaningful impact, demonstrates the skills and behaviors required at the next grade, models our culture and core values, and is prepared to take on broader or more complex responsibilities.  

Criteria 2: Business need refers to a clear, strategic justification for creating or expanding a position based on evolving priorities, organizational goals, or capability gaps. It typically arises when:

Scope or complexity increases beyond what current roles can support.
New initiatives or technologies require specialized skills or leadership.
Organizational alignment calls for clearer ownership or accountability across functions.
Defining the business need ensures that new roles are purpose-driven, aligned with GitHub’s mission, and contribute measurable impact. 

Criteria 3: Budget Availability refers to whether there is approved financial capacity to support a role at a higher grade. Budgets are set annually based on market conditions and are held at the GitHub LT level.  

There is no minimum amount of time a Hubber needs to be at GitHub or in a grade to be promoted; if the promotion criteria above are met, a Hubber is eligible for a promotion.

 

Promotion Process
If a Hubber meets the required promotion criteria, the following steps take place:

Submission of the promotion nomination documentation
Review promotion nominations
Promotion outcome decision
1. Submission of the promotion nomination documentation

Managers complete the promotion submission requirements. The Promotion Justification provides context to the leadership team on the role, Hubber’s readiness and business need.

Grade	Promotion Submission Requirements
Grades 11 & below (IC)	
Promotion Justification in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
Grades 11 & below (manager)	
Promotion Justification in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
Management Excellence Comments in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
Grades 12 + (IC)	
Promotion Justification in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
GitHub Promotion Template (G12+)
Grades 12 + (manager)	
Promotion Justification in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
Management Excellence Comments in Workday. Max 2,000 characters.
GitHub Promotion Template (G12+)
 

2. Review promotion nominations

Once promotion nominations are submitted, each organization reviews the submissions. Managers of managers evaluate the recommendations and select the nominations to be discussed by the organization's leadership team. For promotions at grades 11 and below, the organization leader has final approval authority.

Depending on the size of the organization, broader reviews may be conducted to ensure consistency and alignment across teams. These reviews typically involve managers, their peers, and their management chain. Some organizations may also use a Manager Promotion Review Committee to build consensus on promotion decisions.

Note: The review of promotion nominations into grade 12+ takes place with GitHub’s Leadership Team.

During the reviews, the focus is on:

If the business needs a role at a higher grade with greater scope and job responsibilities leveraging the Job Profile Library.
What success will look like at the higher grade and how it will support achieving the organization’s and GitHub’s goals.
The readiness of the promotion candidate.
How they consistently demonstrated strong impact.
How they modeled our culture and core values (leadership principles, values, manager fundamentals).
Their strengths and areas of development.
Feedback from peers - additional perspectives on readiness, strengths, and development areas.
3. Promotion outcome

Once promotion nomination decisions have been made, updates are submitted to Workday.

If the promotion is approved, the manager will share the news and discuss expectations of the new grade once the snapshot is available. Reflections should be revised to align to updated goals.

If a promotion is not approved, the Hubber’s manager will share relevant feedback and, if appropriate, discuss actionable steps to strengthen future candidacy. Keep in mind that promotion decisions depend on multiple factors, some beyond the Hubber’s or manager’s control.

 

Promotion Conversation
Hubbers should regularly engage in 1:1s and Reflections with their managers to discuss impact, growth, and promotion readiness. These conversations support development and help managers advocate for Hubbers when opportunities arise.

Knowing how to start a conversation about promotions can be challenging. Remember that your manager is your advocate and cares about helping you achieve your career goals. Consider these sample discussion questions to get the conversation started with your manager:

“As I think about my career path, are there any specific actions I can take or things I can learn or work on to get promoted in the future? What are other ways that I can grow in my career?”
“Is there opportunity for advancement (i.e. increased scope/level) within my current job or would there be more opportunity with a different job/team?"
“I would like to focus on __ as I work on growing and developing; will that be helpful in preparing me to be ready for a promotion?”
Continue career conversations with your manager and share your career aspirations. See the Career Fulfillment resources to help you build skills, expand networks, and gain new perspectives.
