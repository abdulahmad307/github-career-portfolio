## Role definition

Assists attorneys and acts as liaison between GitHub attorneys, outside counsel, clients,
organizations, and in- house employees on legal matters. Acts as a front-line support in identifying
triaging issues and risks, escalating where appropriate. Conducts research to support clients on
legal matters. Requests and reviews materials to ensure compliance with applicable laws and GitHub
internal policies. Develops tools, resources, databases, and processes to improve efficiency in work
objectives. Acts as the primary legal contact for business clients, prepares related materials, and
counsels clients. Leads projects within practice group.
