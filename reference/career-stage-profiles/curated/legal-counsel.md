## Role definition

Acts as a legal contact for a product, product feature, sales team, business team, or corporate
function. Executes and resolves legal matters. Serves as a contributor to clients and colleagues by
providing actionable advice on legal and related business matters. Advises clients and the business
on regulatory and compliance requirements. Creates audience-appropriate, clear, and concise legal
documents, communication, and training documents to achieve client goals. Monitors and anticipates
legal risks in the client's business, technology, and/or public policy, and develops solutions to
mitigate future risk. Creates and maintains relationships with appropriate internal and external
stakeholders to escalate issues, drive decisions, and support strategic business goals. Structures,
drafts, and negotiates transactional documents. Provides continuous feedback, contributes to the
improvement of project operational efficiencies, and provides recommendations to improve
efficiencies within the work group and for the delivery of legal services to clients.
