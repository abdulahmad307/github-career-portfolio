## Role definition

Develops readiness content based on stakeholder and organizational needs and distributes product
materials to cross-functional stakeholders across the organization. Facilitates online learning
workshops to improve stakeholder readiness. Suggests areas for improvement and efficiency across
multiple functions and implements system changes. Prioritizes critical problems to solve based on
business needs and strategic impact. Determines the appropriate solution to problems to enable
revenue teams. Implements sol utions at scale across the organization and across various stakeholder
groups. Seeks out strategic relationships with cross -functional stakeholders to align efforts and
promote program success. Creates program management best practices. Understands the shared
enablement efforts within the Microsoft and GitHub ecosystem. Prioritizes stakeholder needs in line
with GitHub strategy and business plans. Creates new project designs and timelines based on changes
to organizational priorities, resources, structure, and/or other roadblocks. Drives stakeholder
involvement in enablement efforts. Acts as a trusted subject matter expert across business groups,
organizations, and levels by providing guidance on commerce changes, product offerings, and audience
insights. Id entifies and develops strategic relationships with internal change management
counterparts, area leadership and stakeholders, and partners. Sets leading and lagging business
metrics to correlate enablement projects with business outcomes. Tracks efforts ag ainst team's key
performance indicators and leverages collected Customer Satisfaction scores to inform future
solutions and initiatives.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Follows procedures for identifying existing readiness materials that need to be updated or created; updates readiness materials under supervision. Supports the facilitation of instructor-led workshops and training sessions. Learns and develops foundational knowledge and awareness around GitHub product offerings, organizational functioning, and sales operations. |
| P2 | G7 | Identifies existing readiness materials that need to be updated or created; creates and updates readiness materials under supervision. Facilitates instructor-led workshops and training sessions with a team member. Develops existing knowledge and awareness of GitHub product offerings, organizational functioning, and sales operations to promote enablement efforts. |
| P3 | G8 | Identifies existing readiness materials that need to be updated based on critical organizational changes; updates learning materials independently. Leads instructor-led workshops and training sessions under general guidance. Assists enablement efforts by continuing to grow and develop knowledge around product offerings, organizational functioning, and sales operations. |
| P4 | G9 | Updates existing critical and time-sensitive readiness materials following critical organizational changes to product offerings or protocols. Facilitates instructor-led workshops and training sessions to improve stakeholder readiness, coaches team members on facilitation, and acts as a partner with organizational leaders. |
| P5 | G10 | Anticipates strategic and time-sensitive updates to existing readiness materials following critical organizational changes to product offerings or protocols. Facilitates non-standard instructor-led workshops and training sessions with broad impact on stakeholder readiness and the organization. |
| M4 | G9 | Coaches team members to update existing readiness materials following organizational changes to product offerings. Supervises the facilitation of instructor-led workshops and training sessions. |
| M5 | G10 | Ensures the continuous update of learning materials to reflect product updates. Works with team members to create instructor-led workshops and training sessions. Coaches senior team members in professional and technical skills to promote innovation; invests in personal professional and technical skill building. |
| M6 | G11 | Ensures stakeholders across the organization have access to updated readiness materials. Ensures instructor-led workshops and training sessions promote strategic goals of the organization. Champions innovation in team members by ensuring continuous skill building in team members. |
