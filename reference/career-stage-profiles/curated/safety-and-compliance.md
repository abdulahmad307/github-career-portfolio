## Role definition

Analyzes and reviews media that includes, but may not be limited to: images, videos, audio, URLs,
and text-based content based on guidelines and policies. Performs content moderation tasks on highly
sensitive material. Checks ticketing queue and content moderation queue to determine their
assignments and prioritization. Assists in leading engagements/projects with support from a manager
or Program Manager. Evaluates guidelines and instructions and identifies where there are
inconsistencies, gaps, and/or opportunities for clarification. Reports issues, articulates a need
for changes and improvements, submits enhancement requests, and builds business justification to
share with tooling teams to support improvement efforts. Maintains active participation and
contributions in team wellness programs, maintains self-awareness and emotional maturity to take
ownership of a personal wellness plan, and contributes to a resilience and wellbeing focused
culture. Influences stakeholders to gain buy-in for projects and initiatives. Understands and tracks
appropriate success criteria and performance metrics (e.g., quality, throughput, and Service Level
Agreement [SLA] Metrics). Responds to internal and/or external reports and escalations by gathering
information on why a decision was made and reports this to the customer.
