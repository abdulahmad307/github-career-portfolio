## Role definition

Manages the execution of the design of talent, structure, culture and/or processes for an assigned
organization. Works closely with functional leaders on organizational workforce planning and team
development. Partners and consults with peer HR job families on leveraging the systems model,
diagnosing talent pyramid and talent management programs, interpreting results, and building
recommendations based on data analyses. Assesses risks and benefits to ensure project feasibility,
and collaborates with business leaders or HR partners to develop effective change-management
strategies and materials. Manages the implementation of the holistic people plan for an assigned
organization by providing insights and recommendations, and shares feedback and ideas with
leadership on the development of people plan. Participates in the implementation of strategic talent
management efforts for an assigned organization by identifying talent strengths and needs and
collaborating with Talent Management. Supports leadership effectiveness and performance by assessing
data, reporting/documenting findings and influencing talent planning and outcomes. Executes on
people and organizational priorities to drive business decisions and achieve specific business
goals.
