## Role definition

Serves as the intermediary and voice for both the customer and the brand. Creates, curates, manages,
and delivers content (e.g., blog posts, videos, images) for products through community channels.
Develops and enforces user guidelines for official community channels for products. Organizes and
runs live community summits and limited online engagements, and partners with internal teams (e.g.,
Events) to help run major live and online events. Coordinates and collaborates with internal and
external stakeholders for ensuring alignment and compliance around community channels and ensuring
community status and health. Manages relationships with influencers and creators to foster and grow
future organic marketing opportunities. Leverages customer research strategies to evaluate community
trends and feedback, and consolidates, interprets, and reports findings.
