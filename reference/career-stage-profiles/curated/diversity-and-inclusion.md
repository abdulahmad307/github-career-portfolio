## Role definition

Develops and keeps the organization abreast of diversity and inclusion policies, relative impacts,
and changes. Ensures communications about priorities, policies, social impact, and initiative
offerings are received and commonly understood. Consults on diversity initiatives and aligns
programs enterprise-wide. Creates resources for internal audience awareness of industry best
practices on diversity and inclusion. Serves as a thought leader internally and externally.
Strategizes execution of and leads implementation for diversity and inclusion activities in
alignment with broader strategy. Builds relationships and leads conversations with stakeholders
enterprise-wide to support planning and layout of solutions as the representative of diversity and
inclusion. Measures the progress and outcomes of diversity and inclusion solutions. Generates action
plans based on results of diversity- related data and recommends changes to leadership for workforce
improvements.
