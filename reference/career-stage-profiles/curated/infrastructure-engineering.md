## Role definition

Builds and enhances the physical and/or virtual frameworks and tools that are used to manage and
maintain the infrastructure. Assesses infrastructure capacity needs, predicts growth trends, and
plans for scalability. Engages with other teams to diagnose and respond to broad incidents. Ensures
the security and compliance of the infrastructure. Establishes appropriate observability and
monitoring of infrastructure to proactively identify and address situations, including instructional
playbooks. Identifies areas for optimization, implements innovative solutions, and drives
initiatives to enhance system performance, reliability, and scalability. Stays updated with latest
technologies, industry trends, and best practices in infrastructure engineering. Understands how to
read and assess common errors to form accurate diagnoses and create or direct fixes. Collaborates
with broader open- source community to enhance existing tools, develop new features, and share best
practices with other infrastructure engineers in the industry. Collaborates with software engineers
and other infrastructure teams to troubleshoot issues and optimize infrastructure performance.
