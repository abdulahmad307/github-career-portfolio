## Role definition

Technical Writing defines, designs, and improves technical content and GitHub Docs through customer research, content strategy, technical accuracy, metrics, feedback, publishing processes, experimentation with content experiences, and cross-functional alignment with product goals.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Learns to engage customers, support strategy execution, follow guidelines, develop technically accurate content for an assigned area, and align content with project goals under supervision. |
| P2 | G7 | Engages customers and feedback under guidance, collaborates on content strategy, develops assigned content, gathers metrics, and begins cross-team collaboration. |
| P3 | G8 | Participates in customer research, addresses feedback, collaborates with stakeholders, develops content for varied users, applies metrics, experiments with delivery formats, and aligns with cross-project stakeholders. |
| P4 | G9 | Seeks partner research opportunities, drives content or GitHub Docs investments, collaborates to educate customers, helps create content principles, shares delivery practices, negotiates partnerships, advocates for telemetry, envisions future content experiences, and drives cross-functional alignment. |
| P5 | G10 | Engages senior partners on research, drives customer engagement planning, owns ecosystem content alignment, directs approval/review processes, socializes content practices across GitHub, influences telemetry, drives experimentation, and leads cross-organizational alignment. |
| M3 | G8 | Helps plan research, ensures customer focus, leads content collaboration, reviews content across areas, uses metrics, and supervises cross-functional partner collaboration, with people-management responsibilities. |
| M4 | G9 | Scopes research, leads customer-focused content collaboration, creates principles, improves content across areas, uses metrics, and manages trusted cross-functional partner relationships. |
| M5 | G10 | Engages partners on ecosystem research, evangelizes work to senior leaders, owns product-experience content alignment, guides cross-functional continuity, advocates telemetry, and drives experimentation. |
| M6 | G11 | Establishes systems and strategic direction for content research, customer focus, ecosystem alignment, review processes, content socialization, telemetry advocacy, experimentation, and cross-organizational collaboration. |

## P4 to P5 evidence signals

| Signal | P4 / G9 evidence pattern | P5 / G10 evidence pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Customer research | Seeks partner research opportunities across a product area or GitHub Docs. | Engages senior partners and drives planning for direct customer engagement. |
| Content strategy | Drives content and/or Docs investments and helps create principles. | Owns content alignment across the product ecosystem and directs review processes. |
| Metrics and telemetry | Advocates for telemetry implementation and uses customer observation to envision future content experiences. | Successfully influences telemetry and drives organization-level experimentation. |
| Cross-functional influence | Negotiates partnerships and aligns content with project goals. | Leads cross-functional and cross-organizational alignment around content strategy. |
| Scaled enablement | Shares information through learning sessions, guides, and informal meetings. | Socializes material across GitHub and leads content-delivery practices at broader scale. |
