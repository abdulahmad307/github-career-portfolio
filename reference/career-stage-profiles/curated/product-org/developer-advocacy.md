## Role definition

Developer Advocacy understands developer communities, earns trust, shares insights and best practices, feeds community signal into product improvement, acts as a subject matter expert for internal stakeholders, scopes technical content and demonstrations, builds technical-expertise systems at scale, and identifies or escalates unaddressed developer issues.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Assists with learning about relevant communities, leverages existing relationships, enters feedback data, supports internal networking, and learns to develop content and demonstrations. |
| P2 | G7 | Supports understanding of communities and begins collecting feedback independently, building trust with internal teams, supporting content and demos, and responding to audience needs. |
| P3 | G8 | Acts as ambassador between technical communities and product/marketing teams, collects feedback, builds trust, scopes content assets, delivers demos, helps upskill communities, and escalates issues with support. |
| P4 | G9 | Deeply understands communities and acts as a trusted ambassador. Seeks out technical-audience feedback, enhances internal trust, develops impactful content and demos, upskills communities, and proactively identifies or escalates unaddressed issues. |
| P5 | G10 | Acts as a known subject matter expert and trusted influential ambassador. Seeks cloud-product feedback, builds critical code/content and demonstrations for community influencers, substantially impacts community growth, and mobilizes resources for highly impactful developer issues. |
| P6 | G11 | Uses advanced knowledge as a subject matter expert and key ambassador. Oversees feedback processes, guides stakeholder navigation, creates executive/community content, identifies partner-to-partner opportunities, and enables successful offer delivery. |
| M4 | G9 | Directs teams on stakeholder relationships, feedback processes, content/demos, community upskilling, and issue escalation, with people-management responsibilities. |
| M5 | G10 | Acts as known SME and thought leader, creates feedback processes across product groups, steers internal/external conversations, guides critical engagements, and mobilizes team resources for critical issues. |
| M6 | G11 | Uses cutting-edge knowledge to lead community understanding, product-group feedback processes, stakeholder navigation, community growth, and partner connections at broader scale. |

| Product feedback loop | Seeks and collects feedback from technical audiences and stakeholders. | Seeks cloud-product feedback and ensures highly impactful issues are addressed or resourced. |
