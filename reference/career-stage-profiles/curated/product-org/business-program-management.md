## Role definition

Business Program Management applies program and project management methods across strategic, complex initiatives. The role creates momentum in matrixed environments, aligns stakeholders, manages risk and conflict, improves business processes, communicates status and strategic direction, and drives rhythm of business for program goals.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Reports status with direction from senior staff, updates process-improvement plans, engages existing stakeholders, and learns the current state of customer experiences and process-efficiency tools. |
| P2 | G7 | Owns timely status reporting and plan updates for assigned areas, engages stakeholders with the right information, and builds understanding of tools, techniques, and strategies for process improvement. |
| P3 | G8 | Applies project management methods to smaller projects, uses tools and methods for timely deliverables, evaluates stakeholder-perspective changes, and analyzes other teams' work for business opportunities. |
| P4 | G9 | Applies advanced understanding of multiple project management methodologies across strategic and complex programs. Defines business and process improvement plans aligned to strategy, ensures stakeholder alignment in high-pressure scenarios, and helps identify future business opportunities. |
| P5 | G10 | Applies in-depth program management across highly strategic and complex programs. Identifies and creates requirements for objectives and tools, actively drives stakeholder discussions, and shapes strategies, solutions, processes, procedures, and mitigation decisions. |
| P6 | G11 | Applies expert program management across highly strategic and complex GitHub or Microsoft programs. Approves requirements, transforms stakeholder engagement relationships, and uses business knowledge and technical expertise to identify future opportunities. |
| M4 | G9 | Oversees cross-functional execution, deliverable quality, stakeholder strategy, business model/value proposition/readiness, and potential business opportunities, with people-management responsibilities. |
| M5 | G10 | Consults across teams and divisions, acts as escalation point to prevent failure or delay, drives stakeholder strategy, and supports customer-experience improvement through new tools, methods, or processes. |
| M6 | G11 | Consults across multiple functions, owns end-to-end quality and business alignment for program lifecycle, evolves stakeholder engagements, and sponsors collaborative innovation. |
