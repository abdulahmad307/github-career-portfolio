## Role definition

Product Operations manages key operations, large projects with organization-wide impact, and stakeholder coordination across several groups. The role uses data from multiple sources to generate actionable insights, sizes change-management plans into concrete components, defines significant end-to-end process changes, and understands software development methodologies at scale.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | G8 | Takes action when operational health deviates beyond acceptable range. Owns small to medium projects autonomously with limited guidance, identifies key operational metrics, considers stakeholder perspectives in strategy and roadmap creation, and learns through incremental change. |
| P4 | G9 | Proactively surfaces recurring operational problems and prioritizes them against the backlog. Manages large projects with organization-wide impact across several stakeholder groups, uses data from multiple sources for actionable insights, sizes change-management plans, calibrates communications to audience needs, and defines significant end-to-end process changes. |
| P5 | G10 | Identifies patterns across operational processes to surface systemic gaps. Independently leads complex reviews, creates Product-wide playbooks, incorporates usage data and operational metrics, role-models collaboration and alignment, drives multi-organization change, crafts the program narrative and path forward, and defines new process solutions. |
| M5 | G10 | Influences operational excellence upstream and downstream through partnerships. Maintains team accountability for company-level projects with executive stakeholders, drives improvements in research methods, oversees change processes, and directs end-to-end process strategy with people-management responsibilities. |
| M6 | G11 | Establishes partnerships and strategic operating mechanisms across upstream and downstream processes. Monitors team accountability for complex company-level work, manages key stakeholder relationships, establishes collaborative cross-functional environments, and directs strategy, process execution, and tool methodology with people-management responsibilities. |
