## Role definition

Product Management identifies customer needs, market opportunities, and investment trade-offs; shapes product/service vision, strategy, and roadmaps; aligns cross-functional partners and senior stakeholders; and evangelizes product direction internally and externally. Scope grows from multiple feature areas to cross-divisional and company-level product strategy.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| M3 | G8 | Leads teams to identify unmet customer needs and market opportunities for feature areas. Coaches teams in product framing, roadmap definition, business impact tracking, cross-functional partner engagement, and product evangelism, with people-management responsibilities. |
| P4 | G9 | Independently identifies unmet or unknown needs and market opportunities across multiple feature areas. Evaluates short- and long-term investment opportunities, owns roadmap areas, influences cross-functional partners and senior stakeholders, and drives scenario walkthroughs before early customer adoption. |
| P5 | G10 | Anticipates unmet needs and market opportunities across multiple feature areas and divisional boundaries. Serves as an expert in long-term investment trade-offs, drives senior stakeholder alignment, owns and drives roadmaps, and leads scenario planning, release criteria, and go-to-market collaboration. |
| P6 | G11 | Identifies complex customer needs and opportunities, brings stakeholders along, and drives company-level product clarity across divisional boundaries. Owns strategy documents and roadmaps, resolves complex alignment issues, contributes portfolio-level solutions, and executes major internal and external evangelism opportunities. |

## P4 to P5 evidence signals

| Signal | P4 / G9 evidence pattern | P5 / G10 evidence pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Customer and market insight | Finds patterns across a portfolio and scopes impact for multiple feature areas. | Anticipates needs across divisional boundaries and uses research evidence to influence prioritization. |
| Strategy and roadmap ownership | Owns roadmap for multiple feature areas and helps define feature set, product framing, and vision. | Owns and drives the roadmap across divisional boundaries and writes/reviews strategy with senior leaders. |
| Investment trade-offs | Identifies data-informed short- and long-term investment opportunities with cross-functional partners. | Serves as an expert in long-term or solution-level investment trade-offs with cross-organizational partners. |
| Stakeholder alignment | Influences internal and external partners, senior leadership, early customers, and key stakeholders. | Drives senior stakeholder engagement and resolves alignment issues across teams or divisions. |
| Scenario planning | Drives walkthroughs and thought exercises to identify problems and contingencies for early adoption. | Leads scenario planning for multiple feature areas and connects release criteria to acquisition, usage, retention, and monetization strategy. |
| External/internal evangelism | Evangelizes feature areas through presentations, blogs, social media, and forums. | Identifies and executes larger evangelism opportunities with stakeholders, partners, analysts, and customers. |
