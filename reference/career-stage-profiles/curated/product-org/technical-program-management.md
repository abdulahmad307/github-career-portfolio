## Role definition

Technical Program Management leads development and implementation for products and services, especially large and complex cross-functional work. The role defines goals, success criteria, schedules, release plans, governance, performance targets, customer supportability, roadmap delivery, and technical programs or product changes that address customer needs and business priorities.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | G8 | Builds relationships within the product family, contributes to program goals and prioritization for feature groups, defines success criteria and metrics, helps coordinate schedules and implementation plans, and validates scenario outcomes with minimal guidance. |
| P4 | G9 | Builds experiences across multiple groups or divisions, defines program goals and prioritization for multiple feature areas, creates relationships for orchestration and integration, partners with engineering on roadmap delivery, manages governance, validates outcomes, and drives customer supportability. |
| P5 | G10 | Demonstrates and builds experiences across multiple groups or divisions, defines goals and metrics, drives orchestration and implementation across multiple feature areas, tracks end-to-end schedules, leads governance, validates outcomes, provides metrics expertise, and leads supportability. |
| P6 | G11 | Acts as subject matter expert for program goals and prioritization across divisional boundaries, delivers multi-year roadmaps, leads end-to-end schedule communication, oversees implementation and governance, and serves as expert for metrics and supportability. |
| M4 | G9 | Directs teams in goals, prioritization, metrics, orchestration, engineering roadmap delivery, schedules, implementation plans, governance, validation, and supportability, with people-management responsibilities. |
| M5 | G10 | Provides expertise and coaching across multiple groups or divisions, guides roadmap delivery and schedules, drives staging and release plans, supports governance leadership, and guides supportability ownership. |
| M6 | G11 | Drives cross-divisional teams through goals, metrics, roadmap delivery, schedules, staging, governance accountability, validation, metrics collection, and customer supportability. |
