## Role definition

Leads the design of a cohesive intervention plan. Builds cases for proposed plans, gains buy-in, and
leverages resources to reach desired goals. Identifies gaps in and recommends strategic actions to
bridge between current and desired states. Leads dialogues about needs and opportunities in
organizational systems to build ongoing strategies around organization, culture, and leadership
topics. Acts as an expert in the application of rigorous diagnostic frameworks and data-based
assessment techniques. Engages with business leaders throughout the organization to gather data and
validate organizational diagnostic findings. Leverages existing organization design frameworks and
owns the process design process to ensure alignment enterprise-wide. Leads the creation of project
plans inclusive of change management, communications, and implementation in partnership with key
stakeholders across the organization. Monitors and analyzes organizational health, culture, and
climate via listening systems.
