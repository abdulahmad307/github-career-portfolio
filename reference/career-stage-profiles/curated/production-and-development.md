## Role definition

Conceives, creates, edits, and readies videos and media for digital distribution on multiple
platforms. Works closely with internal marketing stakeholders and leadership to capture their vision
and determine video needs, advising where videos and media may enhance user experiences across
products, brands, or series of content. Partners closely with the Product Design job family and is
responsible for development and arrangement for production, including outlining assets, sourcing
talent, determining video components, and tracking budgets. Collaborates with crew members to
produce video files using live action or animation resources. Oversees or participates in post-
production editing activities, including adding music, color correction, post-audio, and motion
graphics. Confirms videos and media designed uphold applicable branding and messaging guidelines.
Identifies and uses emerging technologies and techniques to enhance visual media and inform media
strategy. Monitors feedback and insights gleaned from videos and media to understand potential
business impact and customer satisfaction.

| P5 | G10 | Takes requests from and engages with senior executives. Stays up-to-date on production and post-production trends and techniques. Holds responsibility for multiple, high visibility projects or sets of content within a series, handling project management from development through delivery. |
| M5 | G10 | Regularly interfaces and collaborates with business leaders or senior executives. Encourages team members to seek out new production and post-production techniques as well as trends in the technology and consumable media sectors. |
