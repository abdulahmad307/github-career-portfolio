## Role definition

Engages with customers to develop customer understanding, manage relationships, and foster customer
satisfaction to secure renewal of contracts. Plans portfolios and optimizes territories. Manages
renewal processes and monitors/supports consumption goals. Orchestrates and collaborates with others
to execute deals; maintains product and solution expertise to deliver timely and strategic guidance
to current and potential customers.
