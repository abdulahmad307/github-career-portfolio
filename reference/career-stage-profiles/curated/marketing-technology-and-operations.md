## Role definition

Drives large-scale planning and provides solutions by identifying key milestones and developing
plans. Estimates and articulates business impacts and anticipates business outcomes associated with
planning. Oversees business requirement planning documents and leads discussions to ensure action
and execution. Tracks deliverables and timelines and implements processes to support tracking and
quality assurance. Leads the execution of marketing campaigns. Understands the competitive landscape
with regard to Marketing Technology & Operations and GitHub's strategic objectives and adjusts plans
accordingly. Develops and maintains relationships with senior stakeholders across the company.
Communicates with stakeholders to provide clarity and transparency. Develops metrics, targets, and
measurement plans. Conducts operational reporting and monitoring by tracking metric s, identifying
and implementing corrective actions, and communicating issues. Ensures the effective operation of
the lead management system. Executes compliance programs and ensures privacy compliance and process
governance of Marketing products/programs, policies, and across projects.
