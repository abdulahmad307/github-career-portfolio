## Role definition

Integrates programs into current ecosystem. Resolves benefits program issues, process break downs,
and design issues, and benefits opportunities. Operationalizes legal guidance and analyzes market
data. Communicates value of benefits programs and gains buy-in. Leads supplier initial evaluation,
requests for proposals, selection, and onboarding. Maintains or improves vendor performance.
Provides advice and guidance to others for thorough integration during program implementation.
Manages the implementation of a benefits program. Fields feedback, tickets, serves as first touch
point for clients, and reviews suggestions for change from stakeholders. Interacts with internal and
external stakeholders to forecast and mitigate changes to benefits. Manages and adapts a benefits
program encompassing annual processes and collateral resources to support businesses. Delivers
benefits presentations to internal audiences during annual processes and programs. Manages
compliance of benefits programs in alignment with enterprise policies and external compliance
considerations.
