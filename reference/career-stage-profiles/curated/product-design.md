## Role definition

Defines product experiences and collaborates with team members, managers, and developers to shepherd
end- to-end product experiences from concept to ship for products based on user and business needs
and insights. Generates solutions to improve product designs. Creates prototypes and flows for new
products and uses various tools to demonstrate designs to teams, customers, and/or stakeholders.
Collaborates with Engineering for successful design implementation and performs quality checks to
ensure designs are accessible and implemented as intended. Ensures designs meet expectations from
various perspectives (e.g., accessibility, brand, platform user experience), in multiple interfaces
(e.g., desktop, mobile), and with different user configurations and scenarios (e.g., empty states,
edge cases).
