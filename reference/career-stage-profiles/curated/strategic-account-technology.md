## Role definition

Applies business and industry insights, global best practices, proof points from experience across
countries and/or regions, and deep industry expertise to develop recommendations for managing
customer accounts, addressing business challenges for customers, and supporting customers in solving
issues. Applies industry knowledge and expertise in customer businesses and technology platforms to
build digital technology strategy with strategic customers that is aligned with business outcomes.
Partners with strategic customers to provide innovative solutions and integrate GitHub technology in
their businesses. Provides account thought leadership and partners with account executives to set
and drive strategy, own accountability for outcomes, and lead extended teams. Helps to build and
execute on opportunities to improve customers' security posture. Maintains a broad knowledge of
GitHub's product landscape, solutions, and strategy to address customers' needs. Acts as the voice
of the customer and internal advocate by providing insights, feedback, and challenges from the
customer to internal teams. Analyzes customer situations and advises on gaps that would benefit from
GitHub solutions. Acts as a strategic link between GitHub and the customer for identifying pathways
for strategic efforts and resources necessary for building a strategy. Creates, develops, and drives
opportunities for customers to recognize utility and value of GitHub products. Leads the market into
the era of AI and positions GitHub as a leader for the future in AI capabilities. Delivers industry
and technology engagements and/or briefings to provide thought leadership on cutting-edge technology
and drive execution for strategic accounts.
