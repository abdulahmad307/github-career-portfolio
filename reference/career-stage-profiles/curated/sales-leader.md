## Role definition

Leads and executes the go-to-market sales strategy based on customer and partner needs, including
coaching managers to align teams with market strategies, driving sales and revenue growth, and
accelerating Digital & Artificial Intelligence (AI) transformation. Develops sales capabilities and
attracts talent across disciplines. Ensures operational excellence and transformation with a focus
on driving strategic initiatives, addressing business needs, and leading change based on
breakthrough discoveries. Drives customer value realization and satisfaction strategies, managing
executive relationships, and proactively engaging with strategic partners. Leverages industry and
market expertise to pursue industry goals and create strategic relevance with customers. Sets goals
for identifying and exhausting opportunities for developing customer relationships and expanding
growth. Defines and evolves expectations for short-and-long-term sales execution strategies for
driving Digital & Artificial Intelligence (AI) transformation. Builds a positive work culture and
develops people with a focus on inclusion, learning, mentorship, and creating career advancement
opportunities. Drives growth and defines strategies for sales execution through internal
orchestration and collaboration. Develops and leverages external partnerships by building executive
networks, cultivating an ecosystem of partners, and driving continuous engagement and strategic
planning.
