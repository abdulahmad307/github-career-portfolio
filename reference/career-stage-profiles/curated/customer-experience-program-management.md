## Role definition

Proactively identifies potential customer needs and plans for resolution. Guides others to do the
same. Helps to define complex product and program strategy based on customer interactions. Analyzes
customer-centric feedback or conducts research to identify potential drivers of customer friction
and implement solutions. Proactively aligns availability of team resources to address potential
customer issues. Anticipates and addresses complex product/solution-related requests or issues,
leveraging relationships with senior colleagues and/or leaders. Evaluates customer success strategy
and product alignment, identifying acting on gaps and opportunities for improvement. Relays
development opportunities and/or prioritizes customer goals in alignment with internal development.
Supports Engineering, Product, and Customer Success teams in understanding customer interactions to
proactively avoid, minimize, or remove friction and/or pain points. Documents customer or internal
process friction to collect and synthesize insights for process improvement.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P3 | G8 | Strengthens rapport with customers and/or internal stakeholders by providing relevant product advice to meet the needs identified by the business as priorities for improving the customer experience. | P4 | G9 | Acts as a credible point of contact for customers and/or internal stakeholders by providing consistent and relevant product advice to meet the needs identified by the business as priorities for improving the customer experience. | P5 | G10 | Acts as a trusted advisor for the customer and/or internal stakeholders by providing consistent and relevant product advice to meet the needs identified by the business as priorities for improving the customer experience. Leads analysis of customer-centric feedback and research to identify potential drivers of customer friction and implement solutions. |
| M4 | G9 | Leads team to build rapport with the customer and/or internal stakeholders to meet the needs identified by the business as priorities for improving the customer experience. |
| M5 | G10 | Leads the team and stakeholders across the organization to meet the needs identified by the business as priorities for improving the customer experience. |
