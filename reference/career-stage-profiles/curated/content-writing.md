## Role definition

Designs new opportunities to conduct research with cross-functional stakeholders to drive insights.
Maps out strategy to ensure optimal content creation and serve business needs. Applies subject
matter expertise of the customer experience and user interface guidelines and principles when
creating new and unique content to meet user needs. Develops and defines editorial judgment and
data-driven insights/feedback loops to plan and maintain content across different areas. Drives and
leads the implementation of processes for completing, delivering, and distributing writing
workflows. Shares knowledge, best practices, learning opportunities and insights related to the job
family with the broader industry through various mediums. Owns and grows relationships and
expectations with stakeholders to build rapport and credibility.
