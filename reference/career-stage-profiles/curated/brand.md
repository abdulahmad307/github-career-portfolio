## Role definition

Provides coaching and advocacy for brand meaning, storytelling, and experience with internal
partners. Leads ongoing brand perception, research, tracking, and reporting through various types of
studies (e.g., qualitative, quantitative) and gathers customer and community feedback to inform
future strategy. Creates brand messaging architecture and guidelines. Partners on and drives
development of end-to-end assets and frameworks. Aligns brand execution with brand guidelines.
Reviews creative materials and provides brand feedback and guidance. Engages with teams and/or
stakeholders to drive cohesive brand expression across channels, products, devices, and/or
experiences.
