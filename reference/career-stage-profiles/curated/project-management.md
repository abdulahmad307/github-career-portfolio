## Role definition

Provides direction, project management, user perspective, creative vision, and adherence to company
image and quality standards for production and execution of proper sequential processes to complete
the project. Works to determine and/or influence needs and specifications, and uses metrics to
assess content strategy effectiveness and customer satisfaction. Creates and ensures alignment with
schedules, budgets, and resource plans for content production. Coordinates internal and/or external
resources and assets to ensure that content is produced and delivered on time and in accordance with
established requirements. May serve as a liaison with product and program management, product
engineering, design, and marketing teams.
