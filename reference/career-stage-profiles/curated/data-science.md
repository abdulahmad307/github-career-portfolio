## Role definition

Leverages data science knowledge and expertise to analyze problems and project challenges to
uncover, manage, and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines.
Uses querying, visualization, and reporting techniques to explore data, including distribution of
key attributes, relationships between attributes, simple aggregations, properties of significant
subpopulations, and statistical analyses. Leverages machine learning (ML) solutions (e.g.,
classification, regression, clustering, forecasting, NLP, image recognition, explainability AI) and
individual algorithms (e.g., linear and logistic regression, k-means, gradient boosting,
autoregressive integrated moving average [ARIMA], recurrent neural networks [RNN], long short-term
memory [LSTM] networks, large language models [LLM]) to identify the best approach to complete
objectives and generalize ML solutions into repeatable frameworks. Identifies evaluation approaches
and metrics and recommends new methodologies to evaluate models. Tracks advances in industry and
academia, identifies relevant research, and adapts algorithms and/or techniques to drive innovation
and develop new solutions. Develops expertise in proper modeling, coding, and/or debugging
techniques such as locating, isolating, and resolving errors and/or defects. Gathers, integrates,
and interprets information from multiple sources in order to properly troubleshoot errors and/or
defects and identify possible solutions.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Learns project objectives and requirements from a business perspective. Assists team members to acquire data, learning to identify data quality problems, discover insights in the data, and/or detect subsets to form hypotheses. Learns and leverages basic knowledge of various modeling techniques and solutions used within the team. |
| P2 | G7 | Leverages basic knowledge of data science and business to address project challenges and consider factors that can influence outcomes on specific products. Identifies and acquires data necessary for successful completion of project plans with limited guidance. Leverages basic knowledge of machine learning solutions. |
| P3 | G8 | Leverages knowledge of data science and business to identify and address project challenges and uncover important factors that can influence outcomes on specific products. Identifies and acquires data necessary for successful completion of project plans. Leverages working knowledge of machine learning solutions. |
| P4 | G9 | Leverages data science expertise and knowledge of the business to analyze problems and project challenges to uncover, manage, and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines. Leads data acquisition efforts and ensures data is properly formatted and accurately described. |
| P5 | G10 | Ensures objectives of the team are aligned with business needs and shaped by a business perspective. Oversees data acquisition efforts and ensures data is properly formatted and accurately described. Leverages broad and deep knowledge of machine learning (ML) solutions. |
| M4 | G9 | Leverages data science expertise, knowledge of the business, and organizational resources to analyze problems and project challenges to uncover, manage, and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines. Leverages industry standards and best practices in data analysis to optimize delivery and improve data efficiency within and across teams. |
| M5 | G10 | Leverages data science expertise, knowledge of the business, and organizational resources to analyze cross-functional problems and project challenges to uncover, manage, and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines. |
| M6 | G11 | Establishes process for analyzing problems and addressing project challenges to uncover, manage, and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines. Defines strategy and supports team members by managing activities to construct final datasets from initial raw data, providing expert consultation as needed. |
