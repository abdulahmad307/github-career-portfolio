## Role definition

Engages in user research by creating and driving plans for research studies and/or programs based on
customer problems and needs. Applies various research methods and collaborates with others to
execute research plans. Uses research to address business needs, identify and drive solutions and
business direction, and assess risks associated with future initiatives. Takes a multi-dimensional
approach to measuring user experience health. Synthesizes and analyzes quantitative and qualitative
user data to form insights and drive business decisions. Conducts research and applies customer
feedback to optimize user experience. Communicates research insights to various stakeholders. Drives
impact through influencing and motivating action that manifests in product designs or changes to
user experience.
