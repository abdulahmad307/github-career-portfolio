## Role definition

Assesses and pursues market opportunities and trends for the sales representatives that provide
opportunities for partners/customers to leverage GitHub platforms/products by identifying,
quantifying, and qualifying opportunities. Executes and contributes to designing partner/customer
account strategy to deliver on strategic business objectives. Identifies the appropriate executive-
level stakeholder within customers to connect and work with, in cooperation with the sales
representative in pursuit of new business generation. Participates in discussions with
customers/partners to align and help determine customer/partner needs and desired outcomes for a
customer engagement. Drives the creation of a close plan for select deals. Interprets product usage
reporting to inform prospecting strategy. Contributes to continuous process improvements and in-
flight changes through contributions to the review and analysis of performance for specific
strategic plans. Contributes to the design and execution of relevant projects in alignment with
strategic goals. Leverages domain/industry knowledge to develop creative and innovative solutions
for Sales Business Development stakeholders to improve workflow and results.
