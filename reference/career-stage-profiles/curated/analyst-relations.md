## Role definition

Builds strategy, focusing on long-term planning and regular updates to adapt to changing market
conditions and analyst perspectives. Identifies and tiers relevant industry analysts and develops
plan to effectively build and maintain those relationships. Engages in research processes, ensuring
accurate representation and alignment with business messaging/value prop. Analyzes information from
analyst insights to inform the company and influence research goals as well as GitHub products and
solution areas, synthesizing and disseminating easily consumable insights and actions. Uses research
results (positive or negative) to inform and empower sales. Establishes credibility and productive
working relationships amongst internal/external stakeholders and may serve as trusted advisor.
