## Role definition

Uses market intelligence and customer data to identify opportunities for customer acquisition,
adoption, and renewal of a product and/or solution. Engages with customers, partners and the field
to understand customer needs, inform business opportunities, and mitigate risks. Identifies
competitor strengths and weaknesses to find growth, innovation, and thought leadership opportunities
in the market. Defines product roadmaps, KPIs, and long-term strategic product vision. Creates
customer messaging by identifying key customer reasons to buy, retain, and grow and collaborating
with cross-functional partners. Builds global and integrated Bill of Materials (BOM), influences the
creative direction of demos, and recommends materials for product scripts and sales presentations.
Defines and directs Go-to-Market strategies for customer acquisition, adoption, customer advocacy,
and product renewals to promote revenue growth. Follows strategy to achieve revenue or engagement
growth priorities and metrics. Fosters effective relationships with partners and stakeholders to
lead and deliver differentiated customer value. Follows and recommends best practices to improve
product marketing and business performance.
