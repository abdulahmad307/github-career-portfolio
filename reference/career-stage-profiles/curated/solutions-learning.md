## Role definition

Contributes to the analysis, design, development, evaluation, and management of learning programs
and initiatives by identifying organizational and learning needs, securing resources, communicating
with stakeholders, and prioritizing work. Collects data to see how successfully the content is
meeting pre -established learning objectives for specific deliverables. Uses data to prioritize
analyses to execute on, define capability gaps for the business, improve benchmarking, and execute
on learning priorities th rough learner-centric methodology. Initiates, facilitates, and implements
core learning programs. Evaluates and analyzes ongoing effectiveness of programs. Supports and
escalates customer/stakeholder needs. Organizes systems for documenting, storing, and retrieving
items. Manages information archite cture frameworks and content management processes. Accesses
recent research and content experts in Learning and Development to inform best practices.
