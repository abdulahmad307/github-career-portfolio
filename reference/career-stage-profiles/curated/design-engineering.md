## Role definition

Contributes to the end-to-end creation of high-quality user interfaces, ensuring that design intent
is preserved while considering real-world technical constraints and dependencies. Ships scalable,
accessible, and performant UI solutions, partnering with Product, Engineering, and Design (EPD)
stakeholders during the development lifecycle to meet product vision and release timelines.
Prototypes features of various complexity, participating in architectural discussions for front-end
systems, and communicating technical strategies to various audiences. Leads in quality and craft by
defining success metrics for user experience, accessibility, and performance while establishing
design patterns that elevate overall UI standards. Drives cross- disciplinary collaboration,
leveraging technical knowledge and curiosity to push boundaries and raise the bar for UI craft
across the platform.
