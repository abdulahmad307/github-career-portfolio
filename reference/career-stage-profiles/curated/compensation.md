## Role definition

Identifies and recommends new compensation needs and opportunities, process break downs, and program
design issues. Interprets business needs and compliance requirements in comparison with market
insights. Creates and publishes materials to socialize proposed changes associated with a specific
new or existing compensation program. Facilitates program approval by contributing to business case
development, proposals, and communication strategies. Develops and/or leverages existing tools
(e.g., surveys, polls) to conduct compensation program analyses. Leads compensation programs to
support business and human resources programs. Consults as a subject matter expert to advise and
educate others on compensation programs and processes. Identifies ways to improve compensation
processes and enhance policy execution. Synthesize contextual information and provides customized
solutions based on business need.
