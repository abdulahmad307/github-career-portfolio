## Role definition

Drives large-scale planning and provides solutions by seeking to identify key milestones, providing
expertise into developing plans, and leading team discussions. Manages the budget for marketing
technology. Tracks deliverables and timeliness. Identifies and resolves blockers to ensure
implementation. Develops and maintains relationships with senior stakeholders across the company.
Develops metrics, targets, and measurement plans at the campaign/program level by partnering with
internal teams. Gathers information on moderately complex product scenarios by researching and
interviewing people about impacts of potential changes. Uses business knowledge and technical
expertise to assist with analyzing the work of the Marketing team to evaluate the effectiveness of
current marketing efforts and optimizations to determine the optimal business outcomes and user
experiences and identify potential future business opportunities. Oversees the development and
execution of processes and procedures to enable management of the marketing site by providing input
and giving feedback to others. Ensures compliance of Marketing products/programs, policies, and
processes across projects.
