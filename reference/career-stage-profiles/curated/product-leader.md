## Role definition

Drives investment for features or a product based on customer-centric insights and feedback.
Provides guidance to stakeholders to identify product release criteria, customer acquisition, usage,
retention, and monetization strategies. Sets strategy for a product for feature(s)/feature areas
(e.g., product, service) of roadmap. Identifies product investment opportunities as a thought leader
for a Product, evaluating tradeoffs and resources and prioritizing investments. Acts as a leading
Product expert to ensure usability and accessibility for a product. Defines or contributes to the
company business impact for a product with clear success criteria/performance metrics. Leads the
business across an organization to consistently drive and orchestrate strategic initiatives across
their organization, enabling revenue growth by ensuring business and competitive needs are
proactively addressed. Enables teams to enact strategies for organizational agility and provides
recommendations for adjusted strategies and priorities to quickly respond to technical, industrial,
and competitive setbacks. Influences and inspires leaders and teams across the organization to
motivate, engage, and bring teams together.
