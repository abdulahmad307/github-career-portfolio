## Role definition

Designs, plans, executes, and improves upon talent programs to address business needs. Integrates
the GitHub cultural attributes (e.g., diversity, inclusion), leadership principles, and other
talent, learning, frameworks into talent practices. Integrates solutions into other talent
management processes and Human Resources plans. Identifies organizational talent needs, secures
resources, communicates with stakeholders, and prioritizes work to ensure successful program
implementation. Analyzes, and synthesizes people data in partnership with People Analytics to drive
recommendations for talent management enterprise-wide. Acts as a subject matter expert to consult
internal clients on talent management solutions and best practices in the industry. Communicates
talent management approaches (e.g., talent planning, succession planning) to leaders and
stakeholders for education and buy-in to solution implementation. Identifies and addresses skill
gaps in partnership with Learning & Development. Contributes to philosophy, strategy, and standards
to help businesses respond to talent-related opportunities and demands.
