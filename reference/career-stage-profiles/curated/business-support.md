## Role definition

Provides calendar management and time planning support (e.g., cross functional organization-wide).
Serves as support liaison between leader(s) and team(s) to support leader goals in alignment with
leader and organization strategy. Leads the coordination, logistics, and direction for events
aligned with budgetary requirements. Balances portions of projects with varying deadlines, while
maintaining executional and operational excellence within and potentially across
teams/organizations. Inputs, reviews, ensures accuracy, and conducts standard analyses on recruiting
data across workforce planning platforms and processes (e.g., workday, recruiting platforms).
Supports collaborations with Talent Acquisition to drive internal and external recruitment efforts,
and communicates with hiring managers to ensure a smooth process. Oversees standard travel
arrangements (e.g., accommodations, transportation, security) for leaders and/or their immediate
organization.
