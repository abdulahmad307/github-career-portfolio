## Role definition

Advances GitHub strategy to ensure the adoption of shared technology within a
product/solution/service area. Drives and executes the strategic plan and roadmap. Defines
organizational agility and guides the adjusted strategies and priorities to quickly respond to
technical, industrial, and competitive setbacks and/or needs of the ecosystem, partner dynamics, or
incentive structure. Drives a measurable impact within a product/solution/service area and holds
self, as well as senior and executive leaders accountable for changing the direction of the field as
world experts (e.g., inventing something that no one else in the world could, strategic
partnerships, product categories, company policy, tools, methodologies) and creating value for the
company and customers. Establishes planning processes that are flexible and adaptable to accurately
assess progress towards timely completion of work for some high-impact or critical services or
products. Prioritizes and promotes innovation and improvements. Partners with appropriate
stakeholders (e.g., project manager, technical lead) to set the product strategy for some high-
impact or critical services or products. Leverages subject matter expertise to push boundaries,
setting the standards of assessment strategies (e.g., gauging sentiment, A/B testing) to ensure a
growth mindset of learning and a customer-driven culture. Builds and maintains partnerships and
trust with critical partners to influence and identify effective solutions across partners,
competitors, and GitHub. Builds and leverages engineering organization's technical knowledge to
drive the execution of critical technical work. Creates and drives a culture for creating high-
quality and secure products, and continuously integrating and deploying code in simulated, or other
non-production environments to confirm functionality and efficient runtime. Oversees comprehensive
application of automation within production and deployment across some of the highest impact or most
critical products or services. Oversees teams and people strategy and sets baseline expectations to
ensure they maintain a deep understanding of the implications of onboarding new technologies
following expectations of compliance at GitHub. Oversees practices within a product/solution/service
area to ensure cross-organizational clarity and alignment with strategic plans, product goals, and
success metrics, as well as the execution of strategic plans. Programmatically supports an
established culture for producing extensible and maintainable code to identify and address coding
issues and to value effective code reviews for some high-impact or critical services or products.
