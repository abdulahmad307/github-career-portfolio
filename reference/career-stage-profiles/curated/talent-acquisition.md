## Role definition

Understands factors relevant to the business and hiring managers' needs (e.g., time to hire,
location, budget) and uses this knowledge to develop and implement end-to-end hiring plans aligned
to the needs of the business group. Reviews and analyzes data and trends to drive performance, and
uses insights to support other teams. Elicits interest in active and potential candidates by
storytelling. Contributes to the development of new stories. Owns elements of the candidate
experience and prepares candidates for stages within the process. Develops the talent pool by
identifying and engaging candidates and assessing relevant factors. Identifies opportunities to
optimize policies, systems, and processes. Maintains current documentation on candidates'
qualifications and status in the appropriate recruiting or tracking system, within compliance
guidelines. Synthesizes, analyzes, and interprets external market data to inform hiring plans and
implement talent acquisition strategies and initiatives. Proactively assesses and defines
talent/diversity gaps and hiring needs of an organization. Presents data and insights to leaders and
hiring managers to inform hiring plans and talent acquisition strategies and initiatives.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Understands the needs and requirements of the Talent Acquisition team and sets expectations with the client and stakeholders. With guidance, assists in bringing candidates through the recruiting and closing process, and provides support to meet candidate needs. Learns and observes best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories. |
| P2 | G7 | Interprets client's business and requirements and sets expectations with the client and stakeholders. Brings candidates through the recruiting and closing process and provides support to meet candidate needs. Adopts best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories. |
| P3 | G8 | Interprets client's business and requirements to drive a recruiting process to promote an optimal internal and external talent mix. Brings candidates through the recruiting and closing process and works with other teams to meet candidate needs. Implements best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories. |
| P4 | G9 | Interprets client's business and the industry landscape and/or university talent pools to influence the client to adopt optimal recruiting solutions. Closes candidates by anticipating and responding to objections from the business while maintaining fiscal responsibility. |
| P5 | G10 | Uses data and metrics, and an understanding of the long-term business requirements, to advise a business or entity on the implications of talent gaps. Negotiates appropriately with the client to drive solutions that balance their short- and long-term talent needs. Establishes best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories. |
| M3 | G8 | Gathers information on the client's business and requirements to drive a recruiting process to promote an optimal internal and external talent mix. Brings candidates through the recruiting and closing process and works with other teams to meet candidate needs. Applies best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories. |
| M4 | G9 | Uses an understanding of the client's business and the industry landscape and/or university talent pools to influence the client to adopt optimal recruiting solutions. Brings candidates through the recruiting and closing process. Disseminates best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories with his or her own team. |
| M5 | G10 | Leverages data, metrics, and an understanding of the long-term business requirements to advise a business or entity on the implications of talent gaps. Leads business leaders or stakeholders through the recruiting and closing process. Develops best practices on sourcing, recruiting, and/or closing techniques and stories with their own team. |
| M6 | G11 | Applies broad knowledge of the strategic direction across GitHub businesses to develop and advise businesses or entities on long-term, cross-company recruiting strategies focused on critical technical or leadership talent gaps. Oversees the interview and closing process for critical, complex, or high-level candidate searches, and ensures candidate needs are met. |
