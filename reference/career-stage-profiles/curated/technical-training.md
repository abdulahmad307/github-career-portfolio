## Role definition

Executes on skilling offerings for specific audience and learner goals and objectives. Studies skill
-based offerings, fundamentals and/or role-based certifications, or other learning experiences.
Incorporates changes into course content. Works with stakeholders to create alignment on skilling
program goals. Delivers skilling offerings or other learning experiences to target audience. Engages
with learners to ensure content and concepts are being understood and are aligned with learner
goals. Provides knowledge about content areas with content development peers. Promotes and creates
awareness of learning opportunities for GitHub team members. Collaborates with others to promote and
distribute GitHub learning certifications and opportunities. Uses learning and content management
systems to upload, update, and distribute learning and training materials.
