## Role definition

Determines the scope of business and data requirements, source and integrate relevant data, develop
appropriate data frames and analytical solutions, build and leverage connections across business
areas, and articulate connections between business topics, relevant data, and/or analytical
frameworks. Leads discussions with customers and/or stakeholders to interpret analytical results,
develops insights, and articulate pragmatic recommendations that are feasible and account for
potential data limitations. Shares insights, analytical expertise, and relevant data-driven results
through various means, including dashboards, reports, data visualizations, self-service platforms,
slides, internal forums, ad-hoc inquiries, and talking points that synthesize and simplify the most
relevant findings and insights to tell a story that influences senior stakeholders' decision making
and drives business outcomes. Establishes themselves as an expert in data sources and data quality
issues, and shares expertise to reinforce best practices and guide others. Applies expertise in
data, business, and customer needs to determine optimal analytical and inferential techniques. Leads
efforts to automate patterns of ad-hoc analyses into regular reporting features. Ensures alignment
on data definitions and standards across stakeholders to support consistency in data model usage
across stakeholders and/or teams. Drives the adoption of recommended data sources and analytical
best practices to address business needs and deliver key insights and results. Maintains expertise
in data privacy requirements, responsible and ethical data handling practices, and classification
and governance rules and regulations.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | G6 | Begins to learn the business, the data landscape of the business, and the lineage of those data and asks pertinent questions to ensure they understand requirements and/or issues relevant to both the business and its data landscape. Leverages understanding of data and the business to examine projects through a customer- and/or stakeholder-oriented focus. |
| P2 | G7 | Develops depth of understanding of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data across multiple areas. Leverages understanding of data and the business to examine projects through a customer- and/or stakeholder-oriented focus. |
| P3 | G8 | Applies understanding of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data across multiple areas. Applies a customer- and/or stakeholder-oriented focus. |
| P4 | G9 | Applies in-depth knowledge of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data to proactively identify opportunities. |
| P5 | G10 | Applies and shares expertise in multiple aspects of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data to proactively identify ways to explore and address business questions across multiple areas. |
| M4 | G9 | Guides and coaches team members to build an understanding of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data. Leads teams to better recognize and leverage customer and/or stakeholder perspectives when initiating, communicating, prioritizing, and implementing new projects/products. |
| M5 | G10 | Leads and coaches team(s) to apply in-depth knowledge of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of the data. Establishes and implements a customer- and/or stakeholder-oriented focus. Establishes themselves as a trusted expert in data processes and operations, directing team(s) to identify optimal data sources and types to perform analysis on, and directs efforts. |
| M6 | G11 | Drives strategic direction of teams and mentors others to broaden knowledge of the business, its data landscape, and the lineage of those data, and sets expectations for the use and integration of relevant data and trends across multiple sources. Develops and coaches teams on best practices and sets standards for, presenting and sharing insights through various means. |
