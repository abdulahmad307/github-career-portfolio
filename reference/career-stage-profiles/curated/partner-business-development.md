## Role definition

Collaborates with partners to align business models for mutually beneficial relationships. Develops
action plans to deliver tangible solutions for partners. Acts as the voice of the partner and
internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback, opportunities, and challenges from partners
to internal teams. Builds relationships with partners to understand their goals and priorities,
serving as a liaison between the partner and GitHub. Provides analysis of partner needs and advises
on gaps that would benefit from GitHub solutions and gaps that partners can fill for GitHub. Creates
partner plans in alignment with the overall development and partner roadmap. Coordinates teams to
drive sufficient resourcing to capture demands. Sells the vision of GitHub to partners and the value
that can be achieved through partnerships with GitHub, showcasing the value of GitHub's team and
technology to partners and differentiating GitHub from competitors. Provides technical expertise and
shares resources to enable partners to build, go-to-market, and carry a joint message to mutual
customers. Helps partners and their customers be successful with GitHub technology adoption by
helping them realize the value, meet their business objectives, achieve targets, and accelerate
growth. Anticipates partner's technical blockers and executes plans to divert blockers before they
occur.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P2 | G7 | Follows guidance to coordinate a specific scope of work for teams to gather information about the partner. Begins to identify opportunities to act as the voice of the partner and internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback, opportunities, and challenges from the partner to internal teams. |
| P3 | G8 | Coordinates specific scope of work for teams to gather information about the partner, under limited guidance. Acts as the voice of the partner and internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback, opportunities, and challenges from the partner to internal teams. |
| P4 | G9 | Coordinates internal teams to gather information, collaborate on performance markers, and identify potential risks in partner accounts. Acts as the voice of the partner and internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback, opportunities, and challenges from the partner to internal teams. |
| P5 | G10 | Leads the coordination of complex teams across the organization to gather information, collaborate on performance markers, and identify potential risks in partner accounts. Acts as a thought leader and internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback, opportunities, and challenges from the partner to internal teams. |
| M5 | G10 | Oversees the coordination of complex teams across the organization, providing guidance on gathering information, collaborating on performance markers, and identifying potential risks in partner accounts. Provides analysis of overall partner needs for some of the largest and most complex accounts. |
