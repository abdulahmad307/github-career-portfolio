## Role definition

Troubleshoots tickets of varying complexity, progressing open tickets with timely, high -quality
replies. Provides training on new topics within a squad, guiding, assisting, and advising new team
members. Fosters and grows strong relationships with customers and acts as a liaison between
customer and Engineering. Works with Engineering to resolve end user issues and develop new GitHub
features. Identifies and gives proactive input on areas of improvement for team processes and/or
customer experience. Shares functional knowledge with the team, including in ticket reviews, ticket
smashes, and general conversation.

## Grade progression summary

| Profile | Grade | Scope signal from profile summary |
|---|---|---|
| P2 | G7 | Handles lowest difficulty tickets without assistance. Handles lowest difficulty tickets without assistance. Represents the needs of the customer in department-wide discussions. Takes steps to learn the product feedback process through observation and inquiry. Takes steps to learn internal processes. |
| P3 | G8 | Regularly handles lower difficulty tickets with minimal or no assistance. May provide training and guidance on an area of expertise within a squad. Advocates for the customer and the team as a priority. Identifies and reports bugs and feature requests based on customer feedback. |
| P4 | G9 | Troubleshoots tickets of varying complexity independently, prioritizing appropriately. Provides training on a variety of topics within a squad. Fosters and grows strong relationships with customers. Works with Engineering to resolve end user issues and develop new GitHub features. |
| P5 | G10 | Manages all work types and ticket levels in their relevant inbox, prioritizing effectively and ensuring oldest and/or most challenging tickets are handled in a timely manner. Provides training on all topics within a squad. Provides proactive support to customers where possible. |
| M3 | G8 | Provides managerial support and guidance to team members handling tickets of low to moderate difficulty and/or complexity. Supports and helps manage training activities carried out by the team. Coaches team to effectively manage customer interactions. Builds and maintains relationships with Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) teams. |
| M4 | G9 | Manages and mentors team members handling tickets of varying difficulty and/or complexity. Promotes and manages training activities carried out by the team. Coaches team members to effectively manage customer interactions and foster successful relationships. Actively builds and maintains relationships with Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) teams. |
| M5 | G10 | Provides broad oversight of ticket work, ensuring effective assignment, prioritization, and completion of tickets within the team. Oversees and helps develop intra-team training processes. Coaches team members to forge strong customer relationships. Actively builds, fosters, and maintains relationships with Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) leaders. |
| M6 | G11 | Develops processes and manages the pipeline for handling tickets. Oversees and develops intra- and inter-team training processes. Ensures team members develop strong and successful customer relationships. Actively builds, fosters, and maintains relationships with key leaders in Engineering, Product, and Design. |
