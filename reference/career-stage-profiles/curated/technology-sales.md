## Role definition

Identifies and engages with customer technical decision makers and influencers while engaging sales
team and helping to execute sales strategy. Engages in partner sell-with scenarios by acting as
liaison between the partner and team and facilitating partner resources and processes throughout the
course of the project. Provides strategic, technical, and partner input based on GitHub or related
Microsoft capability at the account level to contribute to strategy development, leveraging partner,
competitor, or open-source knowledge. Demonstrates and/or oversees demonstrations (e.g.,
architectural design sessions, and proof of concept [POC] sessions) of products, services, and
integration through initial engagements. Grows domain knowledge and practices expertise by
communicating with customers, partners, internal stakeholders, and industry experts to expand
knowledge and improve product awareness. Generates revenue that meets GitHub's product-specific
goals.
