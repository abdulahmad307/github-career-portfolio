## Role definition

Engages with subject matter experts to translate and inform learning solutions that align with the
audience. Creates content strategy for new learning pathways and topics based on research, content
gaps, and existing content performance. Designs appropriate learn ing experiences. Implements
learning programs within or across teams, organizations, or the company. Measures whether identified
needs were solved based on the initial gap analysis and whether learners are satisfied with the
experience. Continues engagemen t with learners through the full lifecycle of a learning experience.
Develops and maintains ongoing relationships with existing stakeholders to support alignment with
learning-related needs. Updates content or learning experiences as needed.

| P4 | G9 | Conducts gap analysis and research to understand complex and cross-organization problems and context. Identifies additional learning tools and approaches that will support learner needs. Guides others in implementing learning programs within or across teams. |
| P5 | G10 | Conducts gap analysis and research to understand complex and company-wide problems and broader context. Demonstrates understanding of what can be done in house versus outsourced. Develops and executes process improvements for the implementation of learning programs within or across teams. |

| M5 | G10 | Guides team to identify the capability gap of learners, understand the experience needed, and ensure learning experiences would solve the right problem. Demonstrates understanding of what can be done in house versus outsourced. Provides long-term strategies for implementing learning programs. |
