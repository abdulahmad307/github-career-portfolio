Summary
Job Family Definition Builds strategy, focusing on long-term planning and regular updates to adapt to changing market conditions and
analyst perspectives. Identifies and tiers relevant industry analysts and develops plan to effectively build and
maintain those relationships. Engages in research processes, ensuring accurate representation and alignment with

research goals as well as GitHub products and solution areas, synthesizing and disseminating easily consumable

credibility and productive working relationships amongst internal/external stakeholders and may serve as trusted
advisor.
