Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Information Technology
Job Family: Application Product Management
Job Family Definition Manages GitHub's business applications and systems, such as HRIS, revenue, and financial systems. Analyzes, maintains, and
enhances existing applications and systems to ensure operational excellence and meet new technical requirements. Ensures a
reliable and efficient infrastructure housing GitHub's business systems. Collaborates with application users to review business
processes and reviews and defines technical and / or data requirements for application enhancements. Establishes and
communicates system offerings and standards to the business.
