Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Human Resources
Job Family: HR Operations
Summary
Job Family Definition Analyzes, interprets, and reports on employee data with actionable recommendations to support new project
proposals and drive appropriate outcomes with stakeholders. Ensures data quality, security, and integrity by
monitoring and enforcing compliance. Partners with owners of Human Resources programs and other
stakeholders to address employee data change management and governance needs. Performs ongoing
employment reporting and partners with Legal to ensure compliance. Maintains, develops, and advises others on
business processes to enable data management and record keeping. Ensures up-to-date documentation of
processes, desk-top procedures, and knowledge-based content. Supports ongoing key processes by gathering
feedback from stakeholders. Helps identify risks, issues, and barriers in project execution. Identifies the needs for
change, develops change management and communication plans, and leads the implementation and adaptation
to new or changing structures. Evaluates and improves current process management infrastructure in alignment
with data management and compliance. Partners with peer HR job families to manage queries in the queue
management system. Contributes to the supplier/vendor identification, selection, contracting, and on-boarding.
Identifies opportunities to improve vendor performance or further leverage the existing vendor base. Manages
HR system domains and provides insights for project optimizations to reduce costs and improve quality.
