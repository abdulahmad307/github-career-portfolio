Job Family: Business Management
Summary
Job Family Definition Partners with the leadership team to develop and execute planning process and integration across the
organization. Ensures internal continuation of business processes and alignment for strategy initiatives. Cultivates
relationships with leaders and peers of internal teams. Leverages knowledge of the leader's goals and business
approach to ensure business activities are aligned with the leader's objectives. Contributes to cross-team projects
as needed to manage business needs. Develops messaging related to the team's mission and key projects.
Synthesizes findings within workstream and identifies links to broader strategic effort for content and external
facing thought leadership. Helps drive accountability for the organization in executing core business activities.
Individuals in the Technical Business Operations Management specialty also perform responsibilities regarding
integration across the organization, leader support, technical projects, and ensuring organizational health.
