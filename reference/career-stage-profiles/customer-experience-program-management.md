Job Family: Customer Experience Program Management
Summary
Job Family Definition Proactively identifies potential customer needs and plans for resolution. Guides others to do the same. Helps to
define complex product and program strategy based on customer interactions. Analyzes customer-centric
feedback or conducts research to identify potential drivers of customer friction and implement solutions.
Proactively aligns availability of team resources to address potential customer issues. Anticipates and addresses
complex product/solution-related requests or issues, leveraging relationships with senior colleagues and/or
leaders. Evaluates customer success strategy and product alignment, identifying acting on gaps and opportunities
for improvement. Relays development opportunities and/or prioritizes customer goals in alignment with internal
development. Supports Engineering, Product, and Customer Success teams in understanding customer
interactions to proactively avoid, minimize, or remove friction and/or pain points. Documents customer or
internal process friction to collect and synthesize insights for process improvement.

Management M4 (G9)

Management M5 (G10)

impacts on the use of GitHub products. Influences customer business and/or GitHub long-term strategy to remove blockers. Directs the team's focus in understanding and communicating customer
interactions and reinforces proactive avoidance ahead of minimization, or removal of poor customer experience drivers. Establishes the standards for identifying and qualifying if customer experience
is meeting the conditions of success. In addition, this role has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing
performance.
