Job Family: Integrated Marketing
Summary
Job Family Definition Leads the development and execution of marketing strategies including go-to-market (GTM) strategy, plans,
launch activities, customer segmentation, and/or promotional strategies. Drives strategic planning for marketing
campaigns, programs and promotions. Leads and manages digital channel strategy and digital engines. Builds,
manages, and runs cross-channel integrated marketing programs. Drives data-driven decision making and
performance management through measurement and analysis. Manages projects to drive scope, resources,
milestones, and progress.
