Job Family Group: Leader
Job Family: General Leader Guide
General Leader Guide The General Leader Guide is developed to inform consistency across the leadership levels of 12-13 at GitHub for
core expectations, knowledge, skills, and abilities, and qualifications. This leader guide defines common
expectations across problem solving and decision making, scope, functional/technical knowledge, leadership,
influence and collaboration, and role impact across GitHub for each leader level.
