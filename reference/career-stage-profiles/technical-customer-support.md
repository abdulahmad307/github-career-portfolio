Technical Customer Support
P2 (G7)
Handles lowest difficulty tickets without assistance. Handles lowest difficulty tickets without assistance. Represents the needs of the customer in department-wide discussions. Takes steps to learn the
product feedback process through observation and inquiry. Takes steps to learn internal processes. Completes assigned trainings and certifications, or equivalent. Defines the feature set and leads
partnership with Design teams to connect multiple design factors (example, visual design, user experience [UX], application programming interfaces [APIs], hardware design) to desired functionality
and product vision. Owns and drives the roadmap for the multiple feature areas (example, product, service) they own across divisional boundaries. Collaborates with partner team to build golden
configurations and leads and models scenario walkthroughs and thought exercises to identify problems, system friction, and needed contingencies in advance of early customer adoption for multiple
feature areas (example, product, service) across divisional boundaries. Acts as a subject matter expert and collects and includes different views when collaborating with Product Marketing, Business
Planning, and Engineering to identify product release criteria, customer acquisition, usage, retention, and monetization strategies. Executes on major opportunities to evangelize the feature areas
they work on with internal and external stakeholders, partners, analysts, and customers in the industry via presentations, blog posts, social media, and other forums.
Technical Customer Support
P3 (G8)
Regularly handles lower difficulty tickets with minimal or no assistance. May provide training and guidance on an area of expertise within a squad. Advocates for the customer and the team as a
priority. Identifies and reports bugs and feature requests based on customer feedback. Updates various repos (example, team documentation, public documentation, support discussions). Takes
deliberate steps toward becoming a subject matter expert in one or more areas. Supports Voice of Partner (VOP) throughout Engineering rhythm (example, triage, semester planning, roadmap, and
specification review). Responds to requests from partners when they come across issues and areas for improvement in their products/services. Contributes to the creation of packages of reusable
product based on partner/customer experiences that are scaled to multiple partners. Helps identify partner-to-partner opportunities. Supports development of differentiated offers with hands-on
keyboard work via proof of concept (POC).
Technical Customer Support
P4 (G9)
Troubleshoots tickets of varying complexity independently, prioritizing appropriately. Provides training on a variety of topics within a squad. Fosters and grows strong relationships with customers.
Works with Engineering to resolve end user issues and develop new GitHub features. Identifies and gives proactive input on areas of improvement for team processes and/or customer experience.
Shares functional knowledge with the team, including in ticket reviews, ticket smashes, and general conversation. Embeds Voice of Partner (VOP) into Engineering rhythm (example, triage, semester
planning, roadmap, and specification review). Coordinates with partners when they come across issues and areas for improvement in their products/services. Ensures the creation of packages of
reusable product based on partner/customer experiences that are scaled to multiple partners. Helps identify partner-to-partner opportunities and makes connections between regional and global
partners where appropriate. Works within (and across, as needed) their respective engineering organization to get access to correct application programming interfaces (APIs) to enable successful
offer delivery.
Technical Customer Support
P5 (G10)
Manages all work types and ticket levels in their relevant inbox, prioritizing effectively and ensuring oldest and/or most challenging tickets are handled in a timely manner. Provides training on all
topics within a squad. Provides proactive support to customers where possible. Actively gives input on and contributes changes to the Supportability process. Actively strategizes process
improvements for Support alongside management, engages appropriate stakeholders, and follows through on solutions. Represents Support in other teams' Office Hours or meetups as needed. Acts
as the Voice of Partner (VOP) throughout Engineering rhythm (example, triage, semester planning, roadmap, and specification review). Coordinates with partners when they come across issues and
areas for improvement in their products/services. Drives the planning and creation of packages of reusable product based on partner/customer experiences that are scaled to multiple partners.
Independently looks for partner-to-partner opportunities and makes connections between regional and global partners. Works within (and across, as needed) their respective engineering
organization to get access to correct application programming interfaces (APIs) to enable successful offer delivery.
Technical Customer Support
M3 (G8)
Provides managerial support and guidance to team members handling tickets of low to moderate difficulty and/or complexity. Supports and helps manage training activities carried out by the team.
Coaches team to effectively manage customer interactions. Builds and maintains relationships with Engineering, Product, and Design (EPD) teams. Helps strategize process improvements for Support,
engages appropriate stakeholders, and helps team members follow through on solutions. Identifies and evaluates short-term opportunities to enhance the business through cross-group
collaboration and encourages team members to engage. Uses knowledge of the leader's goals and approach to ensure team activities are aligned with the leader's objectives. Supports the leader in
responding to team culture issues, as well as promoting culture initiatives (example, Diversity, Inclusion, and Belonging [DI&B], hiring, onboarding) while partnering with Human Resources (HR) to do
so. Contributes to cross-team projects as needed to meet the changing business environment and manage business needs. Supports the development of content for internal communication.
Leverages manager input to create foundational and storytelling content to articulate strategies, results, and goals for internal and external forums (example, external or customer meetings, executive
meetings, event presentations). In addition, this role has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing performance.

management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing performance.

M6 (G11)
Develops processes and manages the pipeline for handling tickets. Oversees and develops intra- and inter-team training processes. Ensures team members develop strong and successful customer
relationships. Actively builds, fosters, and maintains relationships with key leaders in Engineering, Product, and Design. Drives process improvements for Support, engages appropriate stakeholders,
and ensures team members follow through on solutions. Sets policies and standards that promote general learning and gaining of expertise across specialty areas. Sets priorities for the year and
focus areas for each period, in collaboration with the leader. Leads execution of the vision and direction for organizational culture and health imparted by the leader, such as culture initiatives
(example, Diversity, Inclusion, and Belonging [DI&B], hiring, onboarding) while partnering with Human Resources (HR) leadership to do so. Drives cross-team projects as needed to meet the changing
business environment and to manage complex business needs. Leads executive-level communication with a variety of internal audiences. Designs and oversees the creation of foundational and
storytelling content within the organization and in the industry. In addition, this role has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing
projects, and managing performance.
