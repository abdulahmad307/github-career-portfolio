Summary
Job Family Definition Acts as the voice of their stakeholders, sellers, and customers to design and advocate for plans and strategies that
are customer centric. Owns outcomes related to the development, implementation, communication, and
administration of incentive compensation programs, plans, and policies. Is a trusted advisor for sales and sales
strategy leadership at GitHub, influencing decisions beyond compensation to make their segment and/or solution
area successful. Focuses on continuously improving plans to enable strat egy, striking the right balance between
pushing the envelope to innovate and ensuring that innovations can be implemented to deliver a great seller
experience. Designs compensation plans that attract sellers in the market and retain them in role. Analyzes sales
performance results to create data -driven stories, commission expense reports, or give presentations and
recommendations to stakeholder groups and leadership teams. Partners across the company to develop
trainings, communications, and product feature s that raise awareness to and/or streamline incentive
compensation processes. Promotes seller advocacy across the team and with vendors, working to resolve seller
issues and ad hoc scenarios. Oversees and/or guides vendors to calculate incentive award/comm ission payments,
ensure the feasibility and timeliness of payments, review commission payment reports, maintain and audit
information needed to track revenue., and gather data for analyses.
