Summary
Job Family Definition Develops and oversees the execution of account plans utilizing common sales and delivery methodology (e.g.,
Value Framework, MEDDPICC, Challenger Sale, Command of the Message) for the GitHub sales organization for
multiple accounts and works to ensure engagements yield high volume sales for both GitHub and the customer
that are on track with goals, outcomes, and forecasts. Develops strategies to manage pipeline and meet sales
goals by reviewing forecasts, assessing and mitigating risks, and ensuring strateg y alignment with business
priorities. Works with cross functional teams across GitHub to align on strategies. Identifies deal stakeholders,
mobilizers, and blockers to drive deal momentum and secure sign-off. Engages with internal and external
stakeholders on account planning for assigned accounts and sets strategic priorities and plan to achieve
outcomes. Works with the customer partners to foster trust and brand growth and loyalty through multiple levels
(e.g., managers, executives) of the customer's organization in the assigned accounts. Develops a comprehensive
understanding of customer's business and technology needs and strategies for each assigned account, of their
priorities, and of the industry direction. Involves corporate resources and engages cro ss-industry resources to
drive customer transformation. Leverages relationships with key customer contacts in assigned accounts to
identify and understand the drivers of satisfaction and/or dissatisfaction. Leverages best -in-class sales and
communication techniques to lead virtual teams and key stakeholders with deep industry expertise (e.g.,
customers' sponsors) to build stronger relationships with decision makers of assigned accounts. Develops plans
to offer more targeted solutions that satisfy customers' key performance indicators (KPIs) and align the right
partner solution for customer industry needs. Demonstrates thought leadership and presents business plans to
customers to generate new non-qualified opportunities. Develops and implements plans for maximizing
upselling/cross-selling non-qualified opportunities in certain accounts. Demonstrates ability to position how
GitHub products and solutions will help customers realize innovation through business transformation.
Understands timelines and how to move the pipeline along various stages.
