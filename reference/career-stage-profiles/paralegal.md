Job Family: Paralegal
Summary
Job Family Definition Assists attorneys and acts as liaison between GitHub attorneys, outside counsel, clients, organizations, and in-
house employees on legal matters. Acts as a front-line support in identifying triaging issues and risks, escalating
where appropriate. Conducts research to support clients on legal matters. Requests and reviews materials to
ensure compliance with applicable laws and GitHub internal policies. Develops tools, resources, databases, and
processes to improve efficiency in work objectives. Acts as the primary legal contact for business clients, prepares
related materials, and counsels clients. Leads projects within practice group.

Paralegal
Client Counseling
- Acts as the primary legal contact with assigned business clients and escalates issues to supervisor.
- Makes recommendations and provides support to clients and external counsel on basic legal
matters (e.g., trademark filings) based on understanding of the client's business and subject
matter.
- Participates in meetings with attorneys at the direction of an attorney.
- Prepares presentations and communicates with others across practice area to ensure consensus
and a coordinated voice for client solutions.
- Counsels clients and external counsel and provides status updates on routine issues under the
direction of the attorney.
- Supports the attorney in developing solutions that enable clients to take action towards
achieving business objectives.
- Acts as the primary legal contact for business clients and moderately complex questions.
- Makes recommendations and provides support to clients and external counsel on moderately
complex legal matters (e.g., trademark filings) based on comprehensive understanding of the
client's business and subject matter.
- Conducts meetings with attorneys on routine or assigned matters at the direction of an attorney.
- Prepares presentations and coordinates communication across one's practice area and/or Legal
and Corporate Affairs (CLA) to ensure full alignment and consensus on client solutions.
- Counsels clients and external counsel, resolves conflicts, and provides status updates on
moderately complex issues under some attorney direction.
- Develops and proposes resolutions to the attorney to enable clients to take action towards
achieving business objectives.
Paralegal
Projects and Initiatives
- Collaborates on and contributes to achieving project goals within practice group and team
projects and outside of practice group.
- Leads and demonstrates accountability for small projects within practice group and/or on parts of
larger projects outside of practice group.
- Recommends new programs or processes in response to a business change to meet business
needs.

Client Counseling
- Acts as the primary legal contact and trusted advisor for business clients.
- Provides guidance to paralegals/team members on supporting clients and external counsel on moderately complex legal matters (e.g., trademark filings) based on comprehensive understanding
of the client's business and subject matter.
- Conducts meetings with attorneys on routine or assigned matters at the direction of an attorney.
- Guides others to prepare presentations and coordinate communication with key stakeholders and strategic partners to ensure full alignment and consensus on client solutions.
- Counsels clients and external counsel and guides team to resolve conflicts and provide status updates on moderately complex issues.
- Partners with GitHub compliance leads to identify opportunities, to develop client training on compliance, GitHub policies, and essential legal topics.
- Develops and proposes resolutions to the client to enable clients to take action towards achieving business objectives.
Paralegal
Projects and Initiatives
- Leads and is accountable for a range of projects; identifies roles and responsibilities on collaborative projects or initiative for team members.
- Works across job families with key stakeholders to create new programs or processes in response to a business change to meet business needs.
