Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Engineering
Job Family: Infrastructure Engineering
Job Family Definition Builds and enhances the physical and/or virtual frameworks and tools that are used to manage and maintain the
infrastructure. Assesses infrastructure capacity needs, predicts growth trends, and plans for scalability. Engages
with other teams to diagnose and respond to broad incidents. Ensures the security and compliance of the
infrastructure. Establishes appropriate observability and monitoring of infrastructure to proactively identify and
address situations, including instructional playbooks. Identifies areas for optimization, implements innovative
solutions, and drives initiatives to enhance system performance, reliability, and scalability. Stays updated with
latest technologies, industry trends, and best practices in infrastructure engineering. Understands how to read
and assess common errors to form accurate diagnoses and create or direct fixes. Collaborates with broader open-
source community to enhance existing tools, develop new features, and share best practices with other
infrastructure engineers in the industry. Collaborates with software engineers and other infrastructure teams to
troubleshoot issues and optimize infrastructure performance.

Infrastructure Engineering P1
(G6)
Follows approved processes and/or directions to help build and enhance the physical and/or virtual frameworks and tools that are used to manage and maintain the infrastructure. Learns to assess
infrastructure capacity needs. May engage with other teams to diagnose and respond to certain incidents. Under guidance and following standard processes, helps maintain security and compliance
of the infrastructure. Learns to execute appropriate observability and monitoring of infrastructure to proactively identify and address situations. Helps identify areas for optimization, implements
innovative solutions under guidance, and participates in initiatives to enhance system performance, reliability, and scalability. Becomes familiar with new technologies, industry trends, and best
practices in infrastructure engineering. Learns how to read and assess errors to form accurate diagnoses. Learns about open-source projects. Builds connections with software engineers and other
infrastructure teams.
Infrastructure Engineering P2
(G7)

execute appropriate observability and monitoring of infrastructure to identify and address situations. Helps identify areas for optimization, implements innovative solutions, and contributes to
initiatives to enhance system performance, reliability, and scalability. Gains a basic understanding of new technologies, industry trends, and best practices in infrastructure engineering. Learns how to
read and assess errors to form accurate diagnoses and create or direct fixes. Learns to participate in open-source projects. Participates in collaborations with software engineers and other
infrastructure teams to troubleshoot issues and optimize infrastructure performance.
