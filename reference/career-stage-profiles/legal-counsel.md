Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Legal & Corporate Affairs
Job Family: Legal Counsel
Summary
Job Family Definition Acts as a legal contact for a product, product feature, sales team, business team, or corporate function. Executes
and resolves legal matters. Serves as a contributor to clients and colleagues by providing actionable advice on
legal and related business matters. Advises clients and the business on regulatory and compliance requirements.
Creates audience-appropriate, clear, and concise legal documents, communication, and training documents to
achieve client goals. Monitors and anticipates legal risks in the client's business, technology, and/or public policy,
and develops solutions to mitigate future risk. Creates and maintains relationships with appropriate internal and
external stakeholders to escalate issues, drive decisions, and support strategic business goals. Structures, drafts,
and negotiates transactional documents. Provides continuous feedback, contributes to the improvement of
project operational efficiencies, and provides recommendations to improve efficiencies within the work group
and for the delivery of legal services to clients.

Legal Counsel M6 (G11) Acts as the primary legal contact for large or high-impact business groups. Manages key multi-faceted and highly sensitive legal matters with GitHub-wide impact and/or high external visibility. Acts

communicating with key stakeholders, including GitHub senior executives, simplifying and summarizing highly complex/multi-dimensional issues into actionable strategies and recommendations. In
addition, this role has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing performance.
