adjusted strategies and priorities to quickly respond to technical, industrial, and competitive setbacks. Influences

implemented in the future. This includes the ability to look for
opportunities to grow the business and reduce costs, monitor the

distinct competitive advantages, and consider problems and
opportunities from a broad, multi-functional perspective.
