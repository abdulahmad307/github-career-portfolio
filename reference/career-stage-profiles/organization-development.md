Key Responsibilities (Professional/Individual Contributor)
 Organization Development P6 (G11)
Organization Development
Organizational System Consulting
- Contracts, leads, and coaches others on the design of a cohesive intervention plan (e.g., system redesign) for a complex situation/initiative (i.e., involving technical or political
complexity, or involving many interconnected or adaptive systems) across divisions, global regions, or business units.
- Builds and develops cases for proposed plans, gains ownership/agreement for the course of action, and uses internal/external resources to achieve desired goals and outcomes.
- Leads dialogues with teams about needs and opportunities in organizational systems to ensure strong partnerships and the adoption of recommendations.
- Builds ongoing strategies to keep organization, culture, and leadership topics active across the organization throughout the year.
Organization Development
Organizational Diagnosis
- Acts as an expert in the application of rigorous diagnostic frameworks and data-based assessment techniques (qualitative and quantitative).
- Synthesizes information to identify insights, and frames presentation of information to make recommendations and propose Organization Development interventions.
- Drives changes to measurements and tools.
- Identifies gaps, conflicts, and misalignments in the execution between the current and desired states.
- Provides perspectives and recommendations to achieve strategic objectives.
- Engages with business leaders throughout the organization to gather data and validate organizational diagnostic findings.
- Strategizes approaches on how to share findings to different stakeholder groups across the organization.
Organization Development
Organizational Design
- Leverages existing frameworks and/or creates customized organization design frameworks for the business to use.
- Owns the process designs and leads strategically aligned organizational design efforts enterprise-wide to ensure the alignment and/or the enablement of business strategy and
improved business outcomes.
Organization Development
Change Design and Orchestration
- Leads the creation of major, end-to-end, cross-organization, or multi-year project plans inclusive of change management, communications, and implementation in partnership
with key stakeholders across the organization.
Organization Development
Leadership and Team Effectiveness
- Assesses, understands, and designs interventions (e.g., leveraging data, facilitating discussions, uses inclusive approaches, conflict resolution/mediation) that improve leadership
and team effectiveness to achieve strategic objectives across business groups.
- Drives and collaborates with a variety of functions on leadership capability, and supports effective leadership team and/or other decision-making bodies on critical issues.
Organization Development
Strategy Clarity and Alignment
- Provides strategy analysis to understand the implications of strategic choices with business and culture impact.
- Applies deep understanding of business goals, gaps, and opportunities to guide executive teams in creating, translating, applying, and aligning strategy to improve business
results and organizational culture.
- Influences strategic directions and interpretations of strategy.
- Uses business, economic, and psychological models to drive client clarity.
- Leverages current listening systems and other stakeholder engagements to monitor and analyze clarity of strategy and impression of organizational health, culture, and climate.
