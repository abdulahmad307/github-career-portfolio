Job Family Group: Human Resources
Job Family: HR Business Partnerships
Job Family Definition Manages the execution of the design of talent, structure, culture and/or processes for an assigned organization. Works closely
with functional leaders on organizational workforce planning and team development. Partners and consults with peer HR job
families on leveraging the systems model, diagnosing talent pyramid and talent management programs, interpreting results,
and building recommendations based on data analyses. Assesses risks and benefits to ensure project feasibility, and
collaborates with business leaders or HR partners to develop effective change-management strategies and materials. Manages
the implementation of the holistic people plan for an assigned organization by providing insights and recommendations, and
shares feedback and ideas with leadership on the development of people plan. Participates in the implementation of strategic
talent management efforts for an assigned organization by identifying talent strengths and needs and collaborating with
Talent Management. Supports leadership effectiveness and performance by assessing data, reporting/documenting findings
and influencing talent planning and outcomes. Executes on people and organizational priorities to drive business decisions
and achieve specific business goals.

Job Profile Job Profile Summary
HR Business Partnerships P3
(G8)
Executes on the design of talent, structure, culture, and/or processes for an assigned organization, in partnership with other team members. Collaborates with peer HR job families (e.g., Talent
Management, Organization Development, People Analytics) to support organizational effectiveness efforts, including diagnostics and design to improve organizational health; reviews and guides
data analyses and prepares summary reports; learns the systems model and makes data-based recommendations. Implements aspects of the holistic people plan for an organization with the
understanding of business and practical experience. Implements components of talent movement plans for an assigned organization; understands succession needs of a business.
HR Business Partnerships P4
(G9)
Manages the execution of the design of talent, structure, culture and/or processes for an assigned organization; works with functional leaders on organizational workforce planning and team
development; provides feedback and insights to organizational design. Consults with peer HR job families (e.g., Talent Management, Organization Development, People Analytics) on leveraging the
systems model, diagnosing talent pyramid and talent management processes/programs, interpreting results, and building recommendations based on data analyses; contributes to the design and
implementation of the assessments or solutions for organizational effectiveness. Executes the implementation of the holistic people plan for an assigned organization by providing insights and
recommendations. Implements and facilitates the strategic talent plan for an assigned organization; collaborates with Total Rewards to understand the market competitiveness of the client
organization's compensation.
HR Business Partnerships P5
(G10)
Contributes to the design and/or integration of the holistic organizational plan for an assigned organization; collaborates with business leaders to interpret business needs and people priorities;
partners with business to monitor, execute, and continue to evolve and inform the design; work closely with functional leaders on organizational planning and team development. Identifies
trends/themes in results of analyses provided by People Analytics on talent management processes (e.g., attrition, movement, polls, compensation patterns), talent needs, and risks; communicates
findings with the business as well as the broader HR community. Leads the implementation of the holistic people plan. Develops and implements of the talent development plans using business
insights and data to identify potential successors; executes operational plan defined by others to ready successors and place them in the new role; may work with Talent Management/HR Operations
on the onboarding, training, and development of talent.
HR Business Partnerships P6
(G11)
Leads the design and/or integration of the holistic organizational plan (i.e., talent, structure, culture, process) for an assigned organization; partners with HR leaders and/or business leaders to lead
the holistic organizational planning based on business needs. Integrates disparate sources of information together based on the analyses provided by People Analytics; interprets data, identifies
trends/themes; makes connections with the business as well as the broader system and HR community to identify if issues are systemic or acute. Leverages the all-up HR People Plan to build
consistency across the company. Leads the development and implementation of the talent development plans using business insights and data to identify potential successors; executes operational
plan defined by others to ready successors and place them in the new role; may work with Talent Management/HR Operations on the onboarding, training, and development of talent.
HR Business Partnerships M5
(G10)
Collaborates with business leaders to plan for, interpret results of, and recommend optimizations of business needs and people priorities in relations to organization and team development.
Diagnoses and interprets analytics results of talent practices, processes, and structure in partnership with peer HR job families. Identifies and communicates trends/themes in results of analyses
provided by People Analytics on talent management processes talent needs, and risks. Recommends actionable steps in alignment with organizational strategies to senior leaders and leads the
implementation of data-supported plans. Identifies and pursues closure of gaps in current people plan, people programs, and tools. Contributes to change management initiatives and plans in
collaboration with Communications and Business leaders.; Leads implementation of holistic people plans, drives Talent Rhythm of Business across groups, and develops strategic talent management
strategy across organizations. Assesses data and reporting to coach leaders through organizational change and recommend inclusive leadership practices. In addition, this job profile has people
management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing performance.
HR Business Partnerships M6
(G11)
Leads collaborations with leaders to design, plan for implementation, and ensure business goal alignment of the holistic organizational plan (e.g., talent, structure, culture, process) for an assigned
organization. Ensures that organizational health and effectiveness are prioritized in solutions to address organizational gaps. Integrates disparate sources of information together based on the
analyses provided by People Analytics; interprets data, identifies trends/themes, and presents trends to senior leadership. Makes connections between businesses and systems to identify systemic
and/or acute issues and influence the adoption of action plans. Serves as the HR expert across the company/industry. Defines and directs change to new or existing programs while ensuring effective
global change management strategies are developed and enacted. Leads the development and implementation of the talent development plans using business insights and data. Advances
leadership effectiveness and capabilities planning for an organization. In addition, this job profile has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development,
executing projects, and managing performance.
