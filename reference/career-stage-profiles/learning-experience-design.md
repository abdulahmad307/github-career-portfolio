Learning Experience Design
P1 (G6)
Collects data used in conducting gap analyses to help understand problems. Learns how to integrate learner needs into design. Supports team in implementing learning programs. Begins to develop

Learning Experience Design
P2 (G7)
Conducts gap analysis and research in one's area to understand problems. Integrates learner needs into designs. Implements learning programs within teams with guidance. Develops relationships
with existing stakeholders/partners within an audience or business unit to support alignment with learning-related needs. Performs project management duties for a specific project component or
learning experience to ensure learning experience and content design and development progress as needed.
Learning Experience Design
P3 (G8)
Conducts gap analysis and research to understand problems. Identifies learning tools and approaches that will support learner needs. Implements learning programs within or across teams. Develops
and maintains ongoing relationships with existing stakeholders/partners within an audience or business unit to support alignment with learning-related needs. Performs project management duties
for a portfolio of projects or learning experiences and content to ensure learning experience design and development progress as needed.
Learning Experience Design
P4 (G9)
Conducts gap analysis and research to understand complex and cross-organization problems and context. Identifies additional learning tools and approaches that will support learner needs. Guides
others in implementing learning programs within or across teams. Identifies and develops new relationships and maintains existing relationships with key stakeholders/partners across audiences or
business units to represent their learning-related needs. Leads large projects and portfolios of learning experiences and content to ensure learning experience design and development progress as
needed.
Learning Experience Design
P5 (G10)
Conducts gap analysis and research to understand complex and company-wide problems and broader context. Demonstrates understanding of what can be done in house versus outsourced.
Develops and executes process improvements for the implementation of learning programs within or across teams. Exemplifies best practices in identifying and developing relationships with key
stakeholders/partners across audiences, business units, or verticals/industries to represent their learning-related needs. Leads challenging and large projects and portfolios of learning experiences
and content to ensure learning experience design and development progress as needed.
Learning Experience Design
M4 (G9)
Oversees team members conducting gap analysis of learners and research to understand learner needs and ensure learning experiences would solve the right problem both now and in the near
future. Demonstrates understanding of what can be done in house versus outsourced. Leads the development and application of strategies for implementing learning programs. Leads team
members in identification and development of relationships with key stakeholders/partners across relevant audiences, business units, or verticals/industries to represent their learning-related needs.
Navigates team through large projects and portfolios of learning experiences and content to ensure learning experience design and development progress as needed. In addition, this role has
people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects and managing performance.
Learning Experience Design
M5 (G10)
Guides team to identify the capability gap of learners, understand the experience needed, and ensure learning experiences would solve the right problem. Demonstrates understanding of what can
be done in house versus outsourced. Provides long-term strategies for implementing learning programs. Drives the identification and development of relationships with key stakeholders/partners
across relevant audiences, business units, or verticals/industries to represent their learning-related needs. Drives strategic efforts for challenging and large projects and portfolios of learning
experiences and content to ensure learning experience design and development progress as needed. In addition, this role has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth
and development, executing projects and managing performance.
