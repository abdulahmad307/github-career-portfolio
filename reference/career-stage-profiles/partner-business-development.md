Job Family: Partner Business Development
Summary
Job Family Definition Collaborates with partners to align business models for mutually beneficial relationships. Develops action plans to deliver
tangible solutions for partners. Acts as the voice of the partner and internal advocate by providing insights, data, feedback,
opportunities, and challenges from partners to internal teams. Builds relationships with partners to understand their goals and
priorities, serving as a liaison between the partner and GitHub. Provides analysis of partner needs and advises on gaps that would
benefit from GitHub solutions and gaps that partners can fill for GitHub. Creates partner plans in alignment with the overall
development and partner roadmap. Coordinates teams to drive sufficient resourcing to capture demands. Sells the vision of
GitHub to partners and the value that can be achieved through partnerships with GitHub, showcasing the value of GitHub's team
and technology to partners and differentiating GitHub from competitors. Provides technical expertise and shares resources to
enable partners to build, go-to-market, and carry a joint message to mutual customers. Helps partners and their customers be
successful with GitHub technology adoption by helping them realize the value, meet their business objectives, achieve targets,
and accelerate growth. Anticipates partner's technical blockers and executes plans to divert blockers before they occur.
