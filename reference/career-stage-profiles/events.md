Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Marketing
Job Family: Events
Summary
Job Family Definition Understands and translates business requirements, goals, and objectives into emotionally connecting experiences
for specific audience groups. Holds responsibility for the end-to-end execution of GitHub and/or third-party
events and experiences, in alignment with the overall marketing strategy. Plans, leads, and executes a broad
range of live and online experiences to inspire and deepen connections with company brand, increase positive
brand perceptions, optimize business and marketing priorities, and drive alignment and integration across teams.
Defines and implements creative briefs to inform the visual look, style, and end-to-end experience of events.
Targets specific audiences to make quality individual impressions of the company brand, products, and initiatives.
Takes a data-driven approach to measure the landing and impact of events and experiences and to create a
competitive advantage.
