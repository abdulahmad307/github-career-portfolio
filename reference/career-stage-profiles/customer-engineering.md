Summary
Job Family Definition Synthesizes and combines various business and industry insights from their team, global best practices, experience/case
studies with countries and/or regions, and deep industry expertise related to customers and their competitors. Identifies
customer business requirements and matches them to product features that align closely with customer digital
transformation initiatives, ensuring that the technology provided meets their specific needs. Provides technical guidance to
internal teams to position technology while using customer landscape knowledge. Proactively builds and maintains broad
knowledge of GitHub's product landscape, solutions, and strategy in the customer's industry, as well as of business priorities
and potential threats the customers face. Acts as the voice of the customer and internal advocate by providing insights,
feedback, and challenges from the customer to internal teams (e.g., product groups, engineers). Identifies a pathway in
strategic efforts and resources necessary for building a strategy. Contributes to the creation of technological and business
roadmaps for an account. Uses existing and new readiness resources to support enablement plans for customer technology
adoption. Builds content for marketing events. Identifies customer issues and present possible solutions.
