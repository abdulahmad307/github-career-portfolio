Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Information Technology
Job Family: Systems Engineering
Summary
Job Family Definition Works with stakeholders to determine system requirements to be able to design and build for scale while
managing dependencies. Creates, troubleshoots, and reviews code to be able to implement for systems.
Leverages expertise across system features and partners with appropriate stakeholders on project plans and work
items. Monitors systems for reliability and supportability through active live site maintenance, incident response
during interruptions, integration of instrumentation for gathering system telemetry, and conducting related data
analysis. Drives engineering excellence by adhering to compliance standards, partnering with key partners, and
driving continuous improvement across systems.
