Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Security
Job Family: Security Operations Engineering
Summary
Job Family Definition Designs solutions to address identified control issues (e.g., network, identity, application security). Identifies
opportunities and makes recommendations to improve security posture (e.g., streamline processes, automate)
and proactively prevent attacks. Identifies opportunities and creates automation to improve efficiency
and effectiveness, creating new capabilities/solutions in service of security outcomes. Implements and improves
security policy, standards, and best practices, developing and refining plans, security controls, and solutions to
protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of networks, systems, and data. Educates and engages with
others (e.g., internal teams, external partners) to drive consistency and awareness of security risk, best practices,
and standards. Translates technical information to nontechnical audiences to effectively communicate security
risks, collaborating across the organization to address security risks and define new requirements and feature
sets. Recommends and implements mitigation strategies based on trends identified in the analysis of key metrics,
key performance indicators, and other data sources.
