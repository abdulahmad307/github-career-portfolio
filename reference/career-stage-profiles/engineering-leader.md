Job Family: Engineering Leader
Job Family Definition Advances GitHub strategy to ensure the adoption of shared technology within a product/solution/service area. Drives and
executes the strategic plan and roadmap. Defines organizational agility and guides the adjusted strategies and priorities to
quickly respond to technical, industrial, and competitive setbacks and/or needs of the ecosystem, partner dynamics, or
incentive structure. Drives a measurable impact within a product/solution/service area and holds self, as well as senior and
executive leaders accountable for changing the direction of the field as world experts (e.g., inventing something that no one
else in the world could, strategic partnerships, product categories, company policy, tools, methodologies) and creating value
for the company and customers. Establishes planning processes that are flexible and adaptable to accurately assess progress
towards timely completion of work for some high-impact or critical services or products. Prioritizes and promotes innovation
and improvements. Partners with appropriate stakeholders (e.g., project manager, technical lead) to set the product strategy
for some high-impact or critical services or products. Leverages subject matter expertise to push boundaries, setting the
standards of assessment strategies (e.g., gauging sentiment, A/B testing) to ensure a growth mindset of learning and a
customer-driven culture. Builds and maintains partnerships and trust with critical partners to influence and identify effective
solutions across partners, competitors, and GitHub. Builds and leverages engineering organization's technical knowledge to
drive the execution of critical technical work. Creates and drives a culture for creating high-quality and secure products, and
continuously integrating and deploying code in simulated, or other non-production environments to confirm functionality
and efficient runtime. Oversees comprehensive application of automation within production and deployment across some of
the highest impact or most critical products or services. Oversees teams and people strategy and sets baseline expectations to
ensure they maintain a deep understanding of the implications of onboarding new technologies following expectations of
compliance at GitHub. Oversees practices within a product/solution/service area to ensure cross-organizational clarity and
alignment with strategic plans, product goals, and success metrics, as well as the execution of strategic plans.
Programmatically supports an established culture for producing extensible and maintainable code to identify and address
coding issues and to value effective code reviews for some high-impact or critical services or products.

Job Profile Job Profile Summary
Engineering Leader P7 (G12) Identifies customers' unmet or unknown needs for complex situations, influencing stakeholders and other team members while bringing them along, gaining buy in, and marketing opportunities for
enhancements or development and new products/services of multiple feature areas (e.g., product, service) across divisional boundaries via quantitative (e.g., usage, telemetry) and qualitative analyses
(e.g., customer usage, various listening systems, feedback channels of market and industry trends), looks for patterns in the data across a portfolio, and scopes the impact and prioritization of a
problem. Demonstrates and builds experiences to solve unmet user needs across divisional boundaries. Works across multiple feature areas (e.g., product, service) across divisional boundaries.
Defines and evangelizes others around business impact, success criteria, and performance metrics (e.g., Objectives and Key Results [OKRs], Key Performance Indicators [KPIs] such as engagement,
usage, acquisition, revenue, profitability, and in-project features) and fundamentals (e.g., quality, privacy, security, compliance, accessibility, cost of goods sold [COGS]) of multiple features areas (e.g.,
product, service) across divisional boundaries.
Engineering Leader L1 (G12) Defines the strategy for teams owning the roadmap for a product/solution/service area. Drives and consults upon the short-term (e.g., within one year) strategic plan and roadmap for a component
within a product/solution/service area. Creates guidelines and enables teams to enact strategies for organizational agility. Leverages a deep technical understanding of market and industry trends
and provides insights to customers, partners, and organization to help enable growth, create new business, and define a vision to get ahead of competition. Leads collaborative efforts to establish
planning processes to accurately assess progress towards timely completion of work for some high-impact or critical services or products, in coordination with appropriate stakeholders (e.g., project
managers). Provides deep technical expertise for viability of innovation and improvements within a product/solution/service area to ensure optimal products for customers, and re-directing as
needed. Partners with appropriate stakeholders (e.g., project manager, technical lead) to determine and drive adoption of innovations in product strategy for some of the highest impact or most
critical services or products. In addition, this job profile has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing
performance.
Engineering Leader L2 (13) Translates the strategy for teams owning the roadmap for a product/solution/service area. Drives and executes the short-term (e.g., within one year) strategic plan and roadmap for a component
within a product/solution/service area. Defines organizational agility and guides the adjusted strategies and priorities to quickly respond to technical, industrial, and competitive setbacks and/or
needs of the ecosystem, partner dynamics, or incentive structure within product/solution/service area. Stays abreast of market and industry trends from a technical perspective and provides insights
to customers, partners, and organization to help enable growth, create new business, and define a vision to get ahead of competition. Establishes planning processes that are flexible and adaptable
to accurately assess progress towards timely completion of work for some high-impact or critical services or products, in coordination with appropriate stakeholders (e.g., project managers). In
addition, this job profile has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing projects, and managing performance.
Engineering Leader L3 (G14) Defines the strategy for teams owning the roadmap across product/solution/service areas. Drives and executes the long-term (e.g., in two or more years) strategic plan across
product/solution/service areas and across the organization for roadmap and development plan. Ensures organizational agility and leads the adjusted strategies and priorities to quickly respond to
technical, industrial, and competitive setbacks and/or needs of the ecosystem, partner dynamics, or incentive structure across product/solution/service areas. Influences and champions market and
industry trends from a technical perspective and provides insights to customers, partners, and organization to help enable growth, create new business, and define a vision to get ahead of
competition. Establishes planning processes that are flexible and adaptable to accurately assess progress towards timely completion of long-term goals for some of the highest impact or most critical
services or products, in coordination with appropriate stakeholders (e.g., project managers). Prioritizes and promotes innovation and improvements across product/solution/service areas to ensure
optimal products for customers, and re-directing as needed. In addition, this job profile has people management responsibilities including driving employee growth and development, executing
projects, and managing performance.
