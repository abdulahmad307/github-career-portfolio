Job Family Group: Marketing
Job Family: Production & Development
Summary
Job Family Definition Conceives, creates, edits, and readies videos and media for digital distribution on multiple platforms. Works
closely with internal marketing stakeholders and leadership to capture their vision and determine video needs,
advising where videos and media may enhance user experiences across products, brands, or series of content.
Partners closely with the Product Design job family and is responsible for development and arrangement for
production, including outlining assets, sourcing talent, determining video components, and tracking budgets.
Collaborates with crew members to produce video files using live action or animation resources. Oversees or
participates in post-production editing activities, including adding music, color correction, post-audio, and
motion graphics. Confirms videos and media designed uphold applicable branding and messaging guidelines.
Identifies and uses emerging technologies and techniques to enhance visual media and inform media strategy.
Monitors feedback and insights gleaned from videos and media to understand potential business impact and
customer satisfaction.
