Job Family: Government Affairs
Summary
Job Family Definition Designs strategies to engage government policy makers and influencers (at federal and state levels) and establish
partnerships with industry thought leaders that can advocate and advance GitHub's policy, political, and business
priorities. Analyzes legislative public policy issues impacting the company to proactively advance regulatory and
public policy priorities that create a favorable environment for business and innovation in alignment with
GitHub's mission. Gathers intelligence and assists in the preparation of position papers, policy briefings,
presentations, and legislative testimony for pending legislative actions. Partners with Communications to
establish frameworks and craft messages that enhance the company's reputation, and communicates corporate
positions and objectives to enhance coordination with strategic government and industry partners.
