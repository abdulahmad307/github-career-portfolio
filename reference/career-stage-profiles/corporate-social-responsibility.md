Job Family: Corporate Social Responsibility
Summary
Job Family Definition Develops, manages, and/or implements Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) strategic goals, initiatives, and programs,
developing and managing project timelines, budgets, and plans for CSR programs. Partners with leaders to build CSR programs
in alignment with business objectives and gain buy-in for CSR initiatives. Serves as a subject matter expert in area of expertise,
providing training and consultation to ensure products and operations are aligned with GitHub values and CSR initiatives and
compliant with relevant requirements. Communicates GitHub's perspective on CSR issues, participating in internal and external
events to communicate and advocate for GitHub CSR programs and initiatives. Develops and/or manages external partnerships
(e.g., open-source communities, customers, nonprofit, government agencies) to advance CSR programs and maximize impact.
Implements internal change management processes to drive change within GitHub to align with CSR initiatives and meet
customer expectations. Engages with employees to promote widespread involvement in CSR programs, communicate the
purpose and goals of CSR initiatives, and educate employees on CSR activities. Supports and/or shapes GitHub's policy agenda
that addresses societal issues, developing and/or implementing solutions to advance GitHub's CSR agenda.
