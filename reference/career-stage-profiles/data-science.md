Career Stage Profile
Job Family Group: Data & Analytics
Job Family: Data Science
Summary
Job Family Definition Leverages data science knowledge and expertise to analyze problems and project challenges to uncover, manage,
and/or mitigate factors that can influence final outcomes across product lines. Uses querying, visualization, and
reporting techniques to explore data, including distribution of key attributes, relationships between attributes,
simple aggregations, properties of significant subpopulations, and statistical analyses. Leverages machine
learning (ML) solutions (e.g., classification, regression, clustering, forecasting, NLP, image recognition,
explainability AI) and individual algorithms (e.g., linear and logistic regression, k-means, gradient boosting,
autoregressive integrated moving average [ARIMA], recurrent neural networks [RNN], long short-term memory
[LSTM] networks, large language models [LLM]) to identify the best approach to complete objectives and
generalize ML solutions into repeatable frameworks. Identifies evaluation approaches and metrics and
recommends new methodologies to evaluate models. Tracks advances in industry and academia, identifies
relevant research, and adapts algorithms and/or techniques to drive innovation and develop new solutions.
Develops expertise in proper modeling, coding, and/or debugging techniques such as locating, isolating, and
resolving errors and/or defects. Gathers, integrates, and interprets information from multiple sources in order to
properly troubleshoot errors and/or defects and identify possible solutions.
