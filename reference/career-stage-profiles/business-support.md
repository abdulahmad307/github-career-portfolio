Job Profile Job Profile Summary
Business Support P1 (G6) Provides calendar management and time planning support and suggests strategic preliminary recommendations to leaders according to complex processes and specialized
knowledge of leader's priorities and objectives. Begins to acquire executive presence and maintains strong interpersonal relations and network across organizations to enable

maintaining executional and operational excellence within and potentially across teams/organizations. Ensures self is compliant with GitHub Policy to ensure compliance across

opportunities. Brings signals from partners to determine what offers should be created.
Business Support P2 (G7) Provides complex and dynamic calendar management and time planning support and provides strategic recommendations to leaders according to complex processes and
specialized knowledge of leader's priorities and objectives. Leverages executive presence and maintains strong interpersonal relations and network across organizations to enable
their leaders' work. Leads and drives the coordination, logistics, and direction for events aligned with budgetary requirements. Balances projects end-to-end with varying

Business Support team on GitHub Policy to ensure compliance across team/division/organization and escalates issues as needed. Leads and organizes team experience activities.
Oversees detailed and complex travel arrangements. Ensures the creation of packages of reusable product based on partner/customer experiences that are scaled to multiple
partners. Helps identify partner-to-partner opportunities and makes connections between regional and global partners where appropriate. Works within (and across, as needed)
their respective engineering organization to get access to correct application programming interfaces (APIs) to enable successful offer delivery.
