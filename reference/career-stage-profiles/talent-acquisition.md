Job Family Group: Human Resources
Job Family: Talent Acquisition
Summary
Job Family Definition Understands factors relevant to the business and hiring managers' needs (e.g., time to hire, location, budget) and
uses this knowledge to develop and implement end-to-end hiring plans aligned to the needs of the business
group. Reviews and analyzes data and trends to drive performance, and uses insights to support other teams.
Elicits interest in active and potential candidates by storytelling. Contributes to the development of new stories.
Owns elements of the candidate experience and prepares candidates for stages within the process. Develops the
talent pool by identifying and engaging candidates and assessing relevant factors. Identifies opportunities to
optimize policies, systems, and processes. Maintains current documentation on candidates' qualifications and
status in the appropriate recruiting or tracking system, within compliance guidelines. Synthesizes, analyzes, and
interprets external market data to inform hiring plans and implement talent acquisition strategies and initiatives.
Proactively assesses and defines talent/diversity gaps and hiring needs of an organization. Presents data and
insights to leaders and hiring managers to inform hiring plans and talent acquisition strategies and initiatives.
