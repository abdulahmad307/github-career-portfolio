Job Family Definition Provides expert consulting services to managers and employees in one or more focus areas related to HR programs,
processes, and initiatives. Leads the diagnostics and resolution of issues with increasing complexity and risk related to policy
or culture concerns/violations and incident or threat response independently or by collaborating with managers/peers in
Human Resources and Legal job family groups as appropriate. In partnership with peer HR job families and job families
outside of HR, manages/implements scalable capability building projects, programs, and/or other initiatives by leveraging
existing resources/solutions to mitigate duplicated efforts for a business, region, or geography. Leads the design, execution,
implementation, and/or operation of scalable Human Resources programs. Understands and applies GitHub values and
current HR policies regarding managed projects and programs. Advises employees and managers on compliance to policies
and procedures. In partnership with peer HR job families, manages queries coming through the queue management system.
