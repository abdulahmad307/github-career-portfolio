# Workday Reflections at GitHub

Reflections are a structured opportunity for Hubbers to share what they achieved, how they achieved it, what they learned from recent challenges, and what they will focus on next.

## Reflection cycle timing

GitHub follows Microsoft's fiscal year calendar:

- Fiscal year starts July 1 and ends June 30.
- Q1 = July 1 through September 30.
- Q2 = October 1 through December 31.
- Q3 = January 1 through March 31.
- Q4 = April 1 through June 30.
- H1 = Q1 + Q2.
- H2 = Q3 + Q4.

Reflections consistently happen in **April** and **October**.

Use these cycle labels:

| Workday cycle month | Fiscal label | Fiscal context |
|---|---|---|
| April 2026 | FY26 H2 | Q3-Q4 of FY26, January through June 2026 |
| October 2026 | FY27 H1 | Q1-Q2 of FY27, July through December 2026 |

When drafting during a cycle, use the actual Workday reflection period if the user provides it. If not provided, use the fiscal half label above and gather evidence from the start of that half through the current draft date.

## Question 1

**What results did you deliver, and how did you do it?**

Cover:

- **Quantify your impact:** Describe outcomes you achieved using clear, measurable results.
- **Leverage of AI:** Share how you applied AI to enhance productivity or quality, for yourself, your team, or GitHub customers.
- **Security contributions:** Highlight how you prioritized and strengthened security for GitHub and its customers.
- **Culture in action:** Reflect on the behaviors that exemplify GitHub's culture and values.
- **People Management, for people managers:** Explain how you coached for performance, supported growth, modeled culture, and contributed to team and org success.

## Question 2

**Reflect on recent challenges: what did you learn and how did you apply a growth mindset?**

Cover:

- Describe the situation, and why it tested your skills or mindset.
- Describe the actions you took, and how you stayed open to learning or feedback.
- Describe how the experience shaped your thinking or approach to future challenges.

## Question 3

**What are your goals for the upcoming period?**

Cover:

- Define 3-5 SMART goals with a clear focus on business outcomes.
- SMART = specific, measurable, actionable, relevant/realistic, and time-bound.
- Goals should support the development of secure, scalable products and services.
- If you are a people manager, include your people management goal or goals.

## Length guidance

Workday currently does not enforce a character limit for these reflection questions. Drafts should still stay focused and manager-readable.

Recommended working target:

| Question | Recommended length | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 450-650 words | This question carries the most evidence: outcomes, metrics, AI, security, culture, and people management if applicable. |
| Q2 | 225-350 words | One focused challenge with a clear growth arc is stronger than several shallow examples. |
| Q3 | 3-5 SMART goals, roughly 50-90 words each | Goals should be specific enough to guide action without turning into a full project plan. |

Overall target: roughly 900-1,300 words across all three questions. If the draft runs longer, tighten by removing weaker examples, combining similar themes, and making goals more direct. Do not cut sourced evidence that is essential to the user's strongest impact story.
