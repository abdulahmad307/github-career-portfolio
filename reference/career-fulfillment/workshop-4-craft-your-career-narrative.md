Welcome

Welcome to the "Craft Your Career Narrative" workshop!

In this session, you will:

Identify strategic actions to achieve your career fulfillment goals

Explore how to build your network

Develop your career success story

Uncover new career possibilities

You can use this participant packet to capture your reflections, observations, insights, and to-dos moving forward.

Take one strategic step

Activating one small, strategic action can build momentum and help you move forward with more clarity and confidence.

Activity

Identify 1 action you've been considering taking, big or small, related to your career fulfillment. e.g. attending a conference, getting a new certification, volunteering for a project, etc.

Use the reflections below to think about how to make the action you identified strategic so you can work to achieving it:

How will this action fulfill your motivation drivers?

List some drivers that are met by this action.

How will this action build or allow you to apply your skills?

List the skills you will grow or deepen as a result of taking this action.

How will this action expand your visibility or build connections?

List some ways you can expand your visibility.

List some connections this can help you build.

How will this action move you forward in your career fulfillment path?

List some of your goals this will help you achieve.

Build your network

Building a strong, intentional network is key to growth and influence at work. Reflect on who's in your current network, where the gaps are, and how to start filling them.

Activity

Step 1: Write the names of individuals in your network in each circle. Sparse or empty circles will show you where to focus your attention.

Peers - People at your level

Stakeholders - People who influence your work

Influencers - People with a wider reach or decision-making power

Step 2: Spot the gaps

Look across your circles. Where are the gaps? Which relationships do you need to build?

Step 3: Go where the network lives

Now that you've identified the gaps, ask yourself:

Where do these people spend time? e.g. meetings, channels, committees, events

List ways you can start showing up where your network lives or engaging more intentionally

Build your career story

Fill in the brackets below to help draft your career story.

Tip: Use AI to help you draft your career story by copying and pasting the below prompt into Copilot, and replacing the bracketed text with your own inputs.

Sentence 1:

Skills: [insert your top 1-2 strengths], and [insert your key accomplishment or project]

Sentence 2:

Drivers: [insert what motivates or fulfills you], and [insert specific impact or value you bring]

Sentence 3:

Goals: [insert specific skills or knowledge area], and [insert what kind of role or impact you're seeking]

Uncover career possibilities

There's no single path to fulfillment. Some of the most meaningful careers are built through exploration, creativity, and connections you didn't plan for. Stretch your thinking and uncover new career possibilities by combining self-reflection with creative exploration. Reflect on the prompts below to get curious!

Reflection

Combine your skills + interests

What's one combination of your strengths and interests that could lead to a new possibility?

Look beyond your role or industry

What's one industry, department, or type of role you've never explored, but might align with your values or strengths?

Be open to paths that are not linear

What's one "unplanned" opportunity, side step, or interest that's been pulling your attention lately?

Learn through your network

Who's one person you could reach out to this month to learn about a different path or role?
