Step 5: how will you "own" it?

It's your career and you're in the driver's seat, it's up to you to make continuous development a priority and to have a career that fulfills you. Here are some strategies to put into action:

Track your goals in an Individual Development Plan.

Schedule regular check-ins with your manager to review and discuss your action plan items.

Solicit feedback from others or ask your leader to collect feedback on you via the "Get Feedback" feature in Workday.

Make time for individual reflection to gauge progress, evaluate strengths and opportunities, and pivot when needed.
