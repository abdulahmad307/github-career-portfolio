Welcome

Welcome to the "Explore Your Career Landscape" workshop!

In this session, you will:

Explore the options for your career at GitHub

Understand your growth opportunities

Identify ways to align your goals to the organizations

Familiarize yourself with the needs of the industry to increase your skills

Deepen your career experiences and explore other means of fulfillment at GitHub

You can use this participant packet to capture your reflections, observations, insights, and to-dos moving forward.

Explore opportunities across GitHub

Career fulfillment looks different for everyone, and is shaped by values, drivers, and goals. It might mean growing in your current role, exploring a new role at GitHub, or advancing to another career stage.

Understand your growth opportunities

Understand your growth opportunities to explore new roles in the organization that align with your strengths, skills, and interests. Considering future opportunities such as a lateral move, promotion, or a new challenge can help you stay motivated, engaged, and support your growth journey.

Activity

What available roles, projects, committees, or initiatives are you interested in pursuing to help you grow?

What are 1-2 knowledge, skills, or abilities (KSAs) you need to develop for those opportunities?

What are 1-2 actions you can take to develop in those areas?

Activity Reflection

How do the opportunities for growth you identified align with your long-term career goals? What first step can you take today to move closer to that vision?

Identify ways to align your goals to the organization

Aligning your personal goals with GitHub's mission deepens your skills and contributes impact for the company. Here's how you can align your career fulfillment with GitHub's evolving priorities.

Activity

Identify 1 way your career fulfillment goals align with the priorities of the organization.

Identify 1 way the KSAs of your current job profile/a job profile you're interested in, aligns to the priorities of the organization.

Are there any upcoming projects or opportunities that could support your development?

Activity Reflection

What's one action you can take to align your work more closely with what fulfills you and supports the goals of the organization? What support do you need to take action?

Familiarize yourself with the needs of the industry to increase your skills

Continuous learning helps you stay competitive, innovative, and impactful. Whether you're looking to deepen your expertise, pivot into a new role, or prepare for leadership, staying informed and upskilling are key.

Reflection

Identify 1-2 skills you think will be most valuable in your industry 5 years from now.

What's 1 action you can take to acquire those skills?

Deepen your career experiences and explore other means of fulfillment at GitHub

Activity

Review your Career Fulfillment Roadmap from Learning Paths 1 and 2 and reflect on your definition of success and your career goals. Continue to develop your path to career fulfillment by answering the following questions:

How can I build my internal network?

How have other people gotten to where they are?

How/where can I acquire new skills?

How can I search for internal jobs efficiently and effectively?

How will I identify if a project or role is a "fit?"

What is the process for internal transfers?

What other questions do you have to help chart your path to career fulfillment?

Note: Some of the answers to the above questions can be found in this learning path, others in documentation from HR ZenDesk and some you will get through conversation with others.
