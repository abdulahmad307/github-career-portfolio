At GitHub, high performance is about aligning around what matters most and executing together with purpose. We define it as the outcome of empowered teams, aligned priorities, and a culture grounded in continuous learning and feedback.

To help guide Hubbers, we’ve outlined a clear set of principles and expectations that support a high-impact, inclusive, and collaborative work environment.

 

What High Performance Looks Like
1. Deliver Exceptional Results

Set and execute on ambitious priorities that drive transformational business impact.
Align work to clear, meaningful success measures tied to company objectives.
Actively seek and share feedback—proactively, directly, and in real time.
Deliver strong individual results while contributing to the success of others.
2. Work as One GitHub

Lead with a growth mindset, staying open to new ideas and feedback.
Challenge fixed mindsets by listening, collaborating, and focusing on solutions.
Model new ways of working that accelerate value for our customers and developers.
Embrace diverse perspectives to fuel innovation and build better outcomes—together.
3. Act with Rapid Delivery

Be agile: move quickly, make smart bets, and take bold, intentional risks.
Learn continuously and apply new skills to stay ahead in your craft.
Take accountability for setbacks, reflect, and turn them into learning moments.
Leverage others, use agile ways of working, and scale your impact with speed.
 

What This Means for Hubbers
These expectations are designed to foster an environment where individuals and teams can thrive, innovate, and deliver high-impact work in a psychologically safe, supportive culture.

As a Hubber:

You are responsible for meeting performance expectations.
Your impact, results, and behaviors matter.
And your contributions help shape GitHub’s collective success.
