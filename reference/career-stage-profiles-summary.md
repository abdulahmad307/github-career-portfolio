## What's Consistent Across All Roles

Every career stage profile in the Product Org evaluates along the same axis: **scope increases with grade.** The primary differentiator between grades is how broad, complex, and cross-organizational your impact is.

| Grade Range | Typical Scope |
|---|---|
| G6-G8 | Feature-level or team-level work, with guidance |
| G9 | Multiple feature areas, independently owned |
| G10 | Across divisional boundaries, influencing senior leadership |
| G11 | Company-wide, driving vision and strategy |
| G12 | Industry-level thought leadership |

## Role-Specific Grade Progression

### Product Management (G8-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (M3) | Leads teams across multiple feature areas. People management: coaching, empowering teams. |
| G9 (P4) | Independently owns roadmap across multiple feature areas. Starts building frameworks. |
| G10 (P5) | Works across divisional boundaries. Expert-level investment decisions, influences senior leadership. |
| G11 (P6) | Company-wide initiatives. Thought leadership, defines and unites others around product vision. |

**Key responsibilities:** Analysis of Customer and Market Signals, Product/Service Definition, Product/Service Development, Go To Market, Product/Service Performance.

### Product Design (G6-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G6 (P1) | Follows guidance, assists with wireframes and visual designs under direction. |
| G7 (P2) | Starts estimating schedules, creates wireframes with limited guidance. |
| G8 (P3/M3) | Generates ideation solutions, prototypes independently, performs fundamental tasks. |
| G9 (P4/M4) | Leads ideation for moderately complex challenges, communicates product story to specific audiences. |
| G10 (P5/M5) | Leads others to generate solutions for complex challenges, sets context for senior leadership. |
| G11 (P6/M6) | Sets direction for ideation across the team, conveys vision to senior leadership, leads strategy. |

**Key responsibilities:** Define and Plan, Ideate, Prototyping, Design Quality, Visual Design, Interaction Design, Design Systems, Communication, Storytelling, Collaboration.

### Design Engineering (G8-G12)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Ensures design intent is preserved, implements responsive/accessible UI with guidance. |
| G9 (P4) | Surfaces dependencies and trade-offs early, independently ships scalable UI, inspires engineers/designers. |
| G10 (P5) | Identifies cross-team dependencies, leads architectural proposals spanning multiple products, mentors across domains. |
| G11 (P6) | Makes critical architectural calls, establishes technical vision for design-engineering convergence at scale. |
| G12 (P7) | Defines GitHub-wide front-end architecture direction, innovates industry-leading UI strategies. |

**Key responsibilities:** Design Implementation, Prototyping and Feasibility, Collaboration, Quality and Craft, Curiosity and Initiative, Leadership and Talent Management.

### User Research (G8-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Creates and drives research plans for a feature with guidance. Shares findings to activate design/engineering. |
| G9 (P4) | Creates research plans for a product/service with minimal guidance. Drives impact through influencing action. |
| G10 (P5) | Oversees research across product lines. Leads cross-disciplinary efforts, defines success criteria. |
| G11 (P6/M6) | Champions research initiatives with company-wide or industry impact. Sets best practices. |

**Key responsibilities:** Research Design and Method Selection, Research Execution, Sense-Making and Analysis, Sharing and Activating, Customer Definition, Solution Finding, Experience Optimization, Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration.

### Business Program Management (G6-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G6 (P1) | Reports on status, learns project management tools, engages existing stakeholders. |
| G7 (P2) | Ensures process improvement plans are updated, supports project execution. |
| G8 (P3) | Applies project management methodologies to smaller projects, manages change. |
| G9 (P4/M4) | Applies advanced methodologies to strategic, complex programs. Navigates ambiguity. |
| G10 (P5/M5) | In-depth methodologies across highly strategic programs. Creates requirements for objectives and tools. |
| G11 (P6/M6) | Expert methodologies across GitHub or Microsoft. Influences stakeholder relationships. |

**Key responsibilities:** Project/Program Management, Driving Business Impact and Continuous Improvement, Stakeholder Engagement, Thought Leadership.

### Developer Advocacy (G6-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Builds trust with technical audiences, develops impactful content assets, and delivers demos with guidance. |
| G9 (P4) | Acts as a trusted ambassador between communities and GitHub teams, proactively identifies issues, and raises the bar on content and community impact. |
| G10 (P5) | Known subject matter expert, builds code/content for influencers, leads critical demonstrations, and drives substantial platform or community growth. |
| G11 (P6) | Advanced subject matter expert, guides stakeholder strategy, scales feedback systems, and creates partner-to-partner opportunities. |

**Key responsibilities:** Understanding the Tech Audience/Community, Building Trusted Relationships, Content and Demonstrations, Feedback Loops, Community Growth, Issue Escalation.

### Product Operations (G8-G10)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Manages smaller projects autonomously, identifies key metrics, and incrementally learns through change. |
| G9 (P4) | Manages large projects with organization-wide impact, uses data from multiple sources, and defines significant end-to-end process changes. |
| G10 (P5) | Leads complex reviews and playbooks with organization-wide impact, drives changes across multiple organizations, and crafts the program narrative and vision. |

**Key responsibilities:** Operations and Program Management, Project Management and Execution, Program Ownership, Insights, Stakeholder Management, Change Management, Solution Design.

### Technical Writing (G6-G10)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Designs and develops technically accurate content for one or more areas, applies metrics, and collaborates cross-functionally. |
| G9 (P4) | Drives content strategy for a product area, negotiates partnerships, advocates for telemetry, and aligns content with project goals. |
| G10 (P5) | Owns content alignment across the ecosystem, leads socialization of material across GitHub, influences telemetry, and drives experimentation. |

**Key responsibilities:** Customer Research, Customer Engagement, Content Strategy, Content Creation, Metrics and Telemetry, Cross-Functional Collaboration.

### Technical Program Management (G8-G11)

| Grade | Key Differentiator |
|---|---|
| G8 (P3) | Contributes to goals, success criteria, schedules, and implementation planning for a group of features. |

**Key responsibilities:** Opportunity and Solutions Identification, Program Planning and Prioritization, Success Metrics, Orchestration and Integration, Governance, Release Planning, Supportability.

## IC vs. Manager Path

Both paths exist across Product Management, Product Design, User Research, Business Program Management, Developer Advocacy, Product Operations, Technical Writing, and Technical Program Management:

- **IC grades** operate through influence: expertise, cross-team alignment, persuasion
- **Manager grades** operate through teams: coaching, empowering, holding accountable, building culture
- Both require driving business impact, but ICs do it through direct contribution while managers do it through enablement
- Manager roles additionally map to **Manager Fundamentals**: Model culture, Coach for performance, Care for individuals

## How This Connects to the Portfolio

Regardless of your job family:

- **Impact notes** map to the "What You Do" responsibilities specific to your role
- **How I Worked** maps to the "How You Do It" skills and Culture and Values (shared across all roles)
- **Feedback** provides evidence of skills in action: influence, collaboration, customer focus, craft
- **Goals** should be set against the expectations at your current or target grade
- **Promotion cases** should demonstrate sustained impact at the next grade's scope level

The portfolio structure works the same way for all roles. The evidence you collect and the language you use will naturally reflect your discipline.
