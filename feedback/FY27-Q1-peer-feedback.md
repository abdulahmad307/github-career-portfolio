# FY27 Q1 Peer Feedback

Feedback collected during GitHub FY27 Q1 (July through September 2026). Use `/capture-feedback` in Copilot Chat to format and append new entries.

## What Belongs Here

Save feedback while it is fresh. Some entries will become promotion evidence, some will help with 1:1s or reflection, and some will simply help you remember patterns over time.

Source context is there to help you decide later what to keep private, enrich, paraphrase, or reuse. The system should not decide that for you.

The strongest feedback is:

- **Behavioral and specific:** Names what you did, how you did it, or what changed because of your work
- **Verbatim:** Exact quotes carry more weight than paraphrased summaries
- **Tied to outcomes or decisions:** Feedback about your judgment, trade-off navigation, or leadership in a specific situation

Surface-level kudos ("great job!" or "thanks for the help") are still fine to log. Label the signal strength without overthinking it.

Capture feedback the day it happens. A quick note now is better than trying to reconstruct the moment later.
